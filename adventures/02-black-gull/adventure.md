# The Black Gull

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- Harbour-tavern recruitment → a ship chase (track speed, hull and crew morale) → boarding action → a captain's log revealing who really pays the pirates.

## Twist

- A Vareth prince-merchant funds the piracy to drive up prices.

## The choice

Expose the prince, or cut a deal with the captain.

## Ripples

- Expose the prince: Saltreach Compact ally, Gilded Delta enemy.
- Deal with the captain: a pirate ship owes the Order a favour.
