# The Council of Banners

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- A tense council scene shaped by the ledger, with the new crown (if it was forged) on the table → the Ashen Pretender's move (unmasking, assault, or alliance, depending on The Mask and The Forging) → a battle across the city with allied factions arriving → a final confrontation at the throne.

## The choice

The players at the table decide what happens to the new crown: who wears it, who keeps it, or whether it's used at all, no matter which characters they brought. There's no fixed answer; see the ending options in `world/sunfire-crown.md`. If no crown was forged, the Council decides what Calderra becomes without one.

## Ripples

- Realm's Peace 7–10: the Council can reach a peaceful decision; the Pretender's attack is the only fight.
- Realm's Peace 4–6: the Council splits; at least one claimant betrays it during the attack.
- Realm's Peace 0–3: civil war breaks out in the capital.
- Every faction at +2 sends help; every faction at −2 sends trouble.

## Harrow's Ford echoes

*Optional. Drop one if it fits; none of them changes the session.*

- **The north-gate camp** of Ford-folk refugees is the first place the Pretender's fighting reaches, or the first place that rises if bread runs out.
- **Ushra's envoy** (Varn, if he lived this long) can be at the Council. If the heroes stand up and admit the promise at the river was made and broken, that one honest act could swing the clans. It's the "Change" lever in `world/factions/ashen-pretender.md`.
