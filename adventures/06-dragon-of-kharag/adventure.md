# The Dragon of Mount Kharag

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- Escort the late tribute caravan → dwarven hold politics with Thane Stonecrown → climb into the volcano → fight a young red dragon in its lair, or bargain for a new treaty.

## The choice

Kill the dragon, or bargain.

## Ripples

- A new treaty brings the dwarves to the Council as allies.
- A slain dragon makes the Lantern-sworn famous across Calderra.

## DM notes

A young red dragon with lair actions is deadly and memorable at level 9. A clever bargain should be a real way to win.
