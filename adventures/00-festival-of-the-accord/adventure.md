# The Festival of the Accord

*A D&D 5e one-shot. 4 players, level 4, 3–4 hours.* Converted from `festival_of_the_accord_v2.docx`. See `world/dm-notes.md` for lessons learned and the corrected cipher.

> **Premise.** Decades ago, a contract was forged between the people of Millhaven and the Fey of the Verdant Court. Each year the town holds the Festival of the Accord, a public series of trials to identify champions worthy of facing the Fey Challenger. Win, and the contract holds for another year. Fail, and the Fey walk free in the material world: ruining crops, driving animals mad, and sowing chaos across the land for twelve months.

## Session overview

A single day in Millhaven on the eve of the Summer Solstice. The party arrives as strangers, earns their place as champions through three public Revels, equips themselves through a final private trial, rests, and faces the Challenger at sundown.

- **Act One — Morning:** The party arrives and meets MAX at the Broken Antler.
- **Act Two — Midday:** Three public Revels; the crowd tracks approval; the MC declares the champions.
- **Act Three — Afternoon:** Revel Four (the Rogue's trial), privately at the coliseum gate.
- **Rest — Late afternoon:** Short rest by default; long rest if triggered.
- **Act Four — Sundown (or dawn):** The Final Accord against the Psionic Shambling Mound.

### Why this party?

- **A — They answered a public call.** Each responded for their own reason. MAX greets them as hired contractors.
- **B — They intervened on the road.** They stopped Fey mischief on the way; MAX already knows their names. Strongest option.
- **C — The Accord required strangers.** Champions must have arrived within three days, unbidden. The Fey wrote it so; it has to be chance.

## Act One: Arrival in Millhaven

*"The town of Millhaven smells like woodsmoke and roasted meat. Lanterns hang between every building, the streets packed with townsfolk in their finest clothes. Music drifts from somewhere deeper in the square. Banners everywhere read the same two words: THE ACCORD. You've each arrived separately — or perhaps together — drawn here by rumor, obligation, or something harder to name."*

### The Broken Antler

The largest building in Millhaven, packed to the walls. A broad-shouldered man in his fifties works the room: kind eyes, salt-and-pepper beard, a ring of keys at his belt and a key-shaped scar on his left hand. His name is MAX.

**NPC: MAX, the innkeeper ("The Key").** Mid-50s, stocky. Warm and unhurried; speaks like every word has been considered twice. Keeper of the Accord's history; his family has been keymaster for three generations.
- *Cipher connection:* MAX's name is the keyword for the Revel Four ciphers. Don't reveal it.
- *Before the fight:* MAX walks the party to the coliseum and delivers one last piece of intelligence.

**What MAX tells the party:**

*"Every year we hold the Festival. Three public Revels — the crowd watches, the crowd decides. If they choose you, you're the champions. Then there's a fourth trial before the fight, private, just for preparation. And then at sundown you walk into that coliseum."*

*"I'll tell you what I tell every group. The Revels aren't just spectacle. They're how you learn something about what you're going to face. Pay attention. The Challenger tells you what it is before you ever see it. Most parties just don't listen."*

- *What is the Challenger?* "Something old. Something that thinks. It has roots in the Verdant Court but it's not what you'd call a courtier. More like a weapon the Court keeps on a leash."
- *Has anyone ever lost?* "Three times in my lifetime. The town survived. Barely. Last time the harvest failed two years running and the miller lost his mind. Kept saying the grain was whispering to him."
- *Do we have a choice?* "Everyone has a choice. But the Festival starts in an hour and the crowd's already gathering."
- *The key around your neck?* MAX smiles. "A family heirloom."

**If the party fails:** the Fey roam freely for a year. Crops fail, animals go feral or vanish, townsfolk are driven mad. The contract resets after a year, but Millhaven may not survive another.

## Act Two: The three public Revels

### Crowd approval

The crowd starts **Skeptical**, then can move to **Interested** and **Convinced**.

| Revel | Full success | Partial | Failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One: The Blade | Convinced | Interested | Stays Skeptical |
| Two: The Gauntlet | Up one tier | Stays | Down one tier |
| Three: The Witness | Convinced | Interested | Stays or drops |

A Skeptical crowd still gets champions, but the declaration is quieter. No mechanical effect; a different emotional tone.

### Revel One: The Blade and the Bow (Bard, College of Swords — Training Yard)

**Host: SERA, Master of Arms.** Retired duelist, lean, silver-haired. Respects skill, patience and flair. Does not applaud unless it's earned.
*"Swordsmanship is not about striking first. Any fool with a sharp edge can strike first. The art is knowing when not to — letting your opponent believe they have you, and then showing them they never did."*

- **Bout One — The Invitation.** Leave a false opening, draw the opponent in, counter. Performance DC 12, then Athletics or Acrobatics DC 13; both must succeed. Failure: "You fought. You did not fence."
- **Bout Two — The Crowd.** Weave Blade Flourishes in. Performance DC 14; advantage if another PC feeds the moment.
- **Bout Three — The Lesson.** SERA herself. Insight DC 13 to read her openings, then Performance DC 14.

**Reward.** Full (2–3 bouts): advantage on saves against the Challenger's Slam, Engulf and psionic effects. Partial (1): advantage on Engulf saves only. Failure: none; SERA says "Pay attention in that arena to when it draws back."

**Teaches:** invite-and-counter maps to the Engulf tell. MAX echoes SERA before the fight.

### Revel Two: The Gauntlet (full party — Market Square)

**Host: BRAM, the Gauntlet Master.** Barrel-chested former soldier, booming laugh.
*"The Challenger does not care how good you are alone. It cares how you fall apart when things go wrong. Today we find out if you fall apart."*

A stage fails if more than one PC fails.
1. **The Wall.** Athletics or Acrobatics DC 12; beat it by 5 to grant an ally advantage.
2. **The Crossing.** Acrobatics DC 13; failures fall in the mud, may retry with disadvantage.
3. **The Weight.** Two PCs Athletics DC 14 on levers, one Athletics/Acrobatics DC 12 holding the gate, same round.
4. **The Sprint.** Constitution DC 11 each. Full success: the crowd jumps a tier.

**Reward.** Full (3–4 stages): once per round, one PC may Help an ally as a free action. Partial (2): crowd up one tier. Failure (0–1): crowd down one tier.

### Revel Three: The Witness (Wizard, School of Illusion — Town Square)

**Host: VOSS, Fey Emissary.** A flickering will-o'-wisp in a theatrical mask. Speaks in riddles, delighted by confusion.
*"The Fey are not what you see. They are what you don't. Let the illusionist show us — can they find the truth hiding inside the lie?"*

VOSS accuses the Wizard of stealing the mayor's ceremonial pig and cursing the harvest moon, with illusory evidence.
1. **The Phantom Witness** casts no shadow. Investigation DC 12 (the Inquisitive Rogue grants advantage).
2. **The Forged Document** has ageless ink. Arcana DC 13 (a History DC 11 assist grants advantage).
3. **The Physical Object** — the pig has no smell. Sleight of Hand or Performance DC 14 to show the crowd.
4. **Closing Argument** (if 2+ pieces dismantled): Performance or Persuasion DC 14.

**Reward.** Full: *the Irresistible Illusion* — once in the fight, one illusion the Challenger can't disbelieve; it loses its movement and action that turn. Partial or failure: the party learns the Challenger absorbs lightning. (On failure VOSS says: "You looked at the lie. You never looked for where the lie was not.")

### The town's verdict

**MC: ALDRIC, town elder.** Deliberate; does not rush.
- **Convinced:** *"People of Millhaven. You have watched. You have judged. These four have shown you blade and courage, body and mind. They are your champions. The Accord will hold."*
- **Interested:** *"…I ask you now — are they your champions?"* A beat, then clapping builds.
- **Skeptical:** *"…I name them champions — and I ask that you give them what they need to succeed."* MAX afterward: "The crowd's job is to watch. Your job is to win."

## Act Three: Revel Four — The Seven Chests (Rogue, Inquisitive — Coliseum Gate)

**Host: ALDREN, the Lockmaster.** Small, precise, practical.
*"Seven chests. You pick three. Each one is locked. What's inside depends on what you can open — and what you're clever enough to read once you do."* Nudge if stuck: *"The key to every lock in this town has a name."*

Each opened chest holds a columnar-transposition ciphertext keyed with **MAX** (see the corrected method in `world/dm-notes.md`). If asked, MAX confirms it: "My grandfather built this Revel. He liked the idea that the answer was standing in front of them the whole time."

| # | Difficulty | Lock DC | Plaintext | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Easy | 10 | FLEE AT ONCE | Potion of Haste |
| 2 | Easy | 11 | OPEN THE VAULT | Potion of Greater Healing |
| 3 | Medium | 14 | HOLD THE FORT | Scroll of Shield of Faith |
| 4 | Medium | 15 | LOCK THE GATES | Scroll of Entangle |
| 5 | Hard | 17 | LIGHT THE FIRE | Driftglobe (Daylight disrupts Blindsight) |
| 6 | Hard | 18 | WATCH THE GATE | Sentinel Shield |
| 7 | Very hard | 20 | RUN TO THE TREE | Cloak of Elvenkind |

A failed pick doesn't use a choice; each chest can be attempted once.

## The rest

- **Short rest (preferred):** fight at sundown. MAX delivers the Engulf tell here.
- **Long rest (if the Wizard spent 3+ slots in Revel Three):** rest at the Broken Antler; fight at dawn.

## Act Four: The Final Accord

MAX walks them to the coliseum:
*"When it wants to take someone — really take someone — the whole thing pulls tight for just a moment. Like it's breathing in before it strikes. Don't ignore that moment."*
*"The key's been in my family three generations. My grandfather said the first champions to win did it because they paid attention. I hope you did too."* He hands them a small iron key on a chain.

**The Challenger appears:** *"The vegetation here is wrong — too dark, too dense, veins of faintly luminescent color… What rises is not a creature so much as the idea of one — a shambling mass of vines, roots, and wet earth, twelve feet tall… behind your eyes, you feel it: a low, cold pressure, like a thought that isn't yours."*

**Tactics**
- First turn: it observes and takes no action.
- Engulfs the most isolated PC. Always narrate the vines drawing inward first.
- Irresistible Illusion: commit fully; it loses movement and action.
- Psionic Slam on clusters of 2+.
- Psychic Pulse fires below half HP.
- Lightning heals it; narrate it as horror.
- Below 30 HP it Engulfs every turn.

### Psionic Shambling Mound

*Large plant, neutral. Fey Challenger. CR 5 (1,800 XP).*

- **AC** 15 (natural armour) · **HP** 85 / 100 / 110 · **Speed** 20 ft. · **Prof** +3
- **STR** 20 (+5) · **DEX** 8 (−1) · **CON** 20 (+5) · **INT** 6 (−2) · **WIS** 14 (+2) · **CHA** 5 (−3)
- **Immunities** lightning (absorbed), poison · **Resistances** cold, fire
- **Condition immunities** blinded, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, prone
- **Senses** blindsight 60 ft., passive Perception 12 · **Skills** Stealth +2 · **Languages** understands Sylvan and Druidic

**Traits**
- **Lightning Absorption.** Lightning damage heals it instead.
- **Psionic Awareness.** Can't be surprised. A creature starting its turn within 30 ft. makes a DC 13 Wis save or has disadvantage on its next attack. Suppressed for a round after cold or fire damage.
- **Engulf Tell.** Vines contract before Engulf. A PC who succeeded in Revel One, or passes Perception DC 12, notices and can warn one ally; noticing or warned PCs get advantage on the Str save.

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** Two Slams.
- **Slam.** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. 2d8 + 5 bludgeoning. On a crit, the target is knocked prone.
- **Engulf.** Moves into a Large or smaller creature's space; DC 14 Str save or engulfed (grappled, escape DC 14; restrained; blinded; can't breathe; 2d8 + 5 at the start of each of its turns). Up to two at once.
- **Psionic Slam (Recharge 5–6).** 15-ft. cone, DC 13 Int save; 3d6 psychic and stunned until end of next turn, half and not stunned on a success. Not on the same turn as Engulf.
- **Psychic Pulse (triggered).** First time below half HP: creatures within 30 ft. make a DC 14 Wis save or are frightened until the end of their next turn.

No legendary resistance or legendary actions.

## Endings

**Victory:** *"The creature does not scream. It simply… recedes… Somewhere beyond the treeline, something enormous exhales. The contract holds."* MAX: *"Good. My grandfather would have liked you."*

**Failure:** the Fey walk through into the world, unhurried. Not the end of the story: the town survives the first year; whether it survives the second depends on renegotiating the Accord.

**XP:** 1,800 for the Mound; +400 each for full success on all three public Revels; +100 per successful trial otherwise. **MAX's key** is a campaign hook: it opens something MAX has never told anyone about.
