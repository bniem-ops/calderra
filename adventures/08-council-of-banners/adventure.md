# The Council of Banners

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- A tense council scene shaped by the ledger → the Ashen Pretender storms the capital → a battle across the city with allied factions arriving → a final duel at the throne.

## The choice

The players at the table decide who wears the Crown, or whether anyone does, no matter which characters they brought.

## Ripples

- Realm's Peace 7–10: the Council can reach a peaceful decision; the Pretender's attack is the only fight.
- Realm's Peace 4–6: the Council splits; at least one claimant betrays it during the attack.
- Realm's Peace 0–3: civil war breaks out in the capital.
- Every faction at +2 sends help; every faction at −2 sends trouble.
