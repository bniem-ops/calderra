## Who's in each wave

| Wave | Enemies | Breaks when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1: The Rush** | 6 orcs + the Hush + the Elder | 4 orcs are down |
| **2: The Ram** | Young giant + 3 escort orcs + 2 javelin orcs (north bank) + both Cinders | The giant drops |
| **3: The Champion** | Varn + 2 Shield-kin + whatever's left of the Cinders | Varn yields, falls or wins the duel |

## The Cinders (the Hush and the Elder)

*Medium humanoids (human), neutral. Host warlocks with grey ash rubbed on their faces.*

| AC | HP | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13 (grey robes) | 52 (8d8 + 16) | 30 ft. |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9 (−1) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 18 (+4) |

**Saves** Wis +4, Cha +7. **Skills** Arcana +4, Deception +7, Insight +4. **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11. **Languages** Common, Orc, and one other. **Challenge** 3 for planning (700 XP each).

**Spellcasting.** Charisma, **spell save DC 15**. Only verbal components. They cast from **uses per day**, not spell slots: tick a box each time, and nothing comes back until tomorrow.

- **Pact of the Banked Fire.** A Cinder can't deal damage, with spells, weapons or anything else.
- **Scatter to Ash.** A Cinder never spends its **last** *dimension door* on anything but escape. It casts it on its turn when **bloodied (26 HP or less)**, when its wave breaks, or when its work is done.
- **A spoken word.** A Cinder that can't speak (*silence*, a gag, a hand over its mouth while grappled), is incapacitated, or is counterspelled **can't escape**. Dropped to 0 HP before its next turn = down.
- **One flame at a time.** One concentration spell each. Damage forces a Con save (+2): DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher.

<details>
<summary><b>The Hush's spells, in full</b></summary>

- **Silence** (2nd level, action, range 120 ft.). A **20-ft.-radius sphere** of total silence. Creatures inside are deafened and immune to thunder damage, and **no one inside can cast spells with verbal components**, including the Hush herself. Concentration, up to 10 minutes: it lasts across waves if nobody breaks it.
- **Hold person** (2nd level, action, range 60 ft.). One humanoid makes a **DC 15 Wisdom save** or is **paralysed**. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the spell on a success. Concentration, up to 1 minute. *Paralysed:* incapacitated, can't move or speak, fails Str and Dex saves, attacks against it have advantage, and hits from within 5 ft. are critical. **Varn won't strike a paralysed creature (Clan Law).**
- **Dimension door** (4th level, action, range 500 ft.). Teleports to a spot it can see or describe. **Verbal component only.** Can bring one willing creature.
- **Stilling Word** (action, Recharge 5–6, range 60 ft.). One creature makes a **DC 15 Wisdom save** or **can't speak** until the end of its next turn: no verbal components.
- **Message** (cantrip, range 120 ft.). Whispers to one creature; only it hears.
- **Minor illusion** (cantrip, range 30 ft.). A sound or a 5-ft. image. Investigation **DC 15** to see through it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The Elder's spells, in full</b></summary>

- **Ash Grip** (action, at will, range 90 ft.). A 10-ft. square of ground or deck becomes **difficult terrain** until the start of the Elder's next turn.
- **Transmute rock** (5th level, action, range 120 ft.). A **40-ft. cube** of nonmagical stone turns to **mud**. Moving through it costs **4 feet per foot**. Any creature on that ground when the spell is cast makes a **DC 15 Strength save**, and so does a creature that enters it for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there. On a failure it **sinks and is restrained**; it can use its action to pull free. **Instantaneous:** the mud stays for the rest of the fight. The timber deck isn't stone; the landing and the piers are. Planks laid over the mud (an action per 10-ft. square) make it safe ground.
- **Confusion** (4th level, action, range 90 ft.). Each creature in a **10-ft.-radius sphere** makes a **DC 15 Wisdom save**. On a failure it can't take reactions and rolls a d10 at the start of each of its turns:

  | d10 | Behaviour |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1 | Moves in a random direction (roll a d8 for direction); no action |
  | 2–6 | Doesn't move or act |
  | 7–8 | Melee attack against a random creature within reach; does nothing if there isn't one |
  | 9–10 | Acts normally |

  It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. Concentration, up to 1 minute. *(A confused PC is still a conscious defender for the Burn track.)*
- **Dimension door**, **message**, **minor illusion**: as the Hush.

</details>

## Conditions that come up

<details>
<summary><b>Paralysed, restrained, prone, and the river</b></summary>

- **Paralysed** (*hold person*): incapacitated, can't move or speak, auto-fails Str and Dex saves, attacks against it have advantage, and hits from within 5 ft. are crits. Varn and the Shield-kin won't attack it.
- **Restrained** (mud): speed 0, attacks against it have advantage, its attacks have disadvantage, disadvantage on Dex saves. An action pulls free of the mud.
- **Prone** (Shield Shove): melee attacks against it have advantage, ranged attacks have disadvantage; standing costs half its movement.
- **In the river:** swept 20 ft. downstream each round; DC 12 Athletics to grab a pier.

</details>
