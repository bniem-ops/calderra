# Harrow's Ford: DM one-pager

*Scroll top to bottom as you run it. Callouts are the story beats you can't skip. Click any ▸ box for the exact numbers. Full text: [`adventure.md`](adventure.md). All enemies: [`stat-blocks.md`](stat-blocks.md).*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The three threads.** Land each one at least once:
> 1. **The river is the line:** Varn's speech, his cord, *"Ask your duchess what Corwen signed."*
> 2. **The Host opened the door:** the grey figures (the Cinders), Fenn's *"they came from the west,"* the boats.
> 3. **Someone told the Host you were coming:** the voice in the fog recites the job letter.

> [!NOTE]
> **The premise in one breath:** the bridge is bread (the mill feeds the wall). The warband must **cross and burn it from the south bridgehead**, because a fire lit from the north gets doused and rebuilt. Hold the landing.

<details>
<summary><b>Prep checklist</b></summary>

- [ ] Job letter printed, one per player
- [ ] One motive question per player (face / brain / sneak / muscle / support)
- [ ] Edge index cards (write the tier on each)
- [ ] Varn's card with a **damage tally** (initials + running totals)
- [ ] Burn track: three boxes
- [ ] Waves loaded in the tracker, both Cinders in all three
- [ ] Map or the physical bridge. *If you use Gemini, start a new chat for this map.*

</details>

## 1. Hook (10 min)

Dusk. The bridge, the mill, torches moving in the village, fog and drums beyond the river.

> [!TIP]
> **The Millhaven carter** is on the south road with Greenhollow rye for the mill (the wall's winter bread). There's a lamp on his cart pole *"for the Lantern-sworn who saved our Festival."* It's a Festival callback; let it land.

## 2. Varn's terms (15 min)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Varn walks to mid-bridge and plants his spear. *"This river is the line. Burn your bridge before dawn and we take nothing south of the water. Keep it, and at first light we come across and burn it for you."*
> **Show the two grey figures** at the fog's edge behind him. They're gone before he reaches them.

<details>
<summary><b>Varn's answers</b> (one question only, from the fog)</summary>

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| "What line?" | "Ask your duchess." |
| "Why now?" | "Because the fence-builders' soldiers have gone home." |
| "Who are the grey ones?" | *(pause)* "Guests." |

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What Varn really wants</b> (play him straight)</summary>

- The March to **accept the river as the border**. The bridge is proof.
- Players burning it themselves = the best outcome for him.
- He has to burn it from the **south** end or it gets rebuilt.
- He won't sneak across tonight: he owes the Host a *fight*, and the Cinders are watching.

</details>

## 3. Rally the Ford (10 min)

> [!NOTE]
> **Edda:** iron-grey braid, stiff knee, tally stick, swears a lot, first onto the bridge. *"That bridge is bread. I'm not burning bread for an orc."* She waves a PC up onto the wagon: *"Say something that isn't swearing."*

<details>
<summary><b>Rally roll</b></summary>

- **Persuasion, Intimidation or Performance, DC 12** (advantage for a good line)
- **Success:** militia starts with **Volley tier 1**
- **Failure:** nothing lost
- Others: muscle hauls barrels, brain counts stores, sneak watches the fog (passive Perception 13+: a grey figure waves back), support finds Fenn

</details>

## 4. The Night Clock (50 min)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Every Edge roll is DC 10.** 9 or lower: nothing (retry in Watch 2) · **10–16: tier 1** · **17+: tier 2**.
> Several PCs on one task: best roll counts; describing how you help gives advantage. **Watch 1:** fixed list, no resting. **Watch 2:** freeform.

<details>
<summary><b>Watch 1 tasks</b> (six tasks, five PCs: something gets left out)</summary>

| Task | Role | Check | Edge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scout the north bank | Sneak | Stealth | Forewarned |
| Barricade the bridge | Muscle | Athletics | Barricade |
| Rig the bridge | Brain | Investigation / tinker's tools | Pitch Trap |
| Drill the militia | Face, support | Persuasion / Intimidation / Performance | Volley |
| Tend the old warden | Support | Medicine | Old Warden's Bow |
| Study the grey ones | Brain | Arcana | Ash-Wise |

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Edge results by tier</b></summary>

| Edge | Tier 1 | Tier 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Forewarned** | Know the wave order; **advantage on initiative** every wave | + **Marked the Cinders:** first attack each round vs a Cinder has advantage |
| **Barricade** | One span north of the landing. **AC 15, 30 HP**, half cover behind it | **50 HP** |
| **Pitch Trap** | Once: 10×20 ft, **DC 13 Dex**, 3d6 fire (half on a save); difficult terrain to end of next round | **Two traps** |
| **Volley** | Once per wave: **2d6** piercing to one enemy, no roll | **3d6** |
| **Old Warden's Bow** | Once per wave: **+5, 1d8+3** from the mill roof | **Twice** per wave |
| **Ash-Wise** | Players learn: Cinders can't harm anyone, flee by a **spoken** spell, are leashed by concentration | + Cinders have **disadvantage on their first concentration save** each wave |

⚠️ An **unsprung pitch trap** still on the deck when the warband takes the landing = the Burn track starts with **one box ticked**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Scouting results</b> (sneak)</summary>

- **9 or lower:** spotted on the way back. Each scout: **Athletics/Acrobatics DC 12** or take **2d6**. Wave 1 gets **advantage on its first attack**.
- **10+:** in and out clean. They see:
  - ~40 orcs, the young giant with a pine-log ram, javelins
  - **Pots of pitch and torches by every fire** (they mean to carry the fire)
  - Young warriors throwing stones in the river for luck
  - **The argument** (thread 2): the Cinders want the bridge *and* the village burned; Varn says *"The river is the line. Not one step past it."* A Cinder: *"Remember whose boats carried you around the fence."*
- **Only if they dig:** the straw helmet painted with a thorn (*"the fence's guard"*).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Fenn</b> (support)</summary>

- Medicine roll = Old Warden's Bow (tier by roll).
- **Says freely (thread 2):** *"They came from the west. From behind the wall."* He saw boats at the river mouth. Nobody believed him.
- **Only if pressed** (Insight DC 14, or shown the straw helmet): he walks the **hollow towers**. *"If they know the towers are empty, the wall's done."* Note whether they keep it secret.

</details>

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Start of Watch 2: the voice in the fog (thread 3).** Fog to the knees, drums stop. A pleasant voice names a PC and recites their job letter: *"...a warband is on the north bank, and the reeve asks for help..."* Then *"Use your judgment,"* with a little laugh. **Don't explain it.** (Edda: "Never showed that letter to a soul.")

<details>
<summary><b>Watch 2 options and the bridge's features</b></summary>

Each PC does one thing: **retry or upgrade an Edge**, **walk the bridge and build a custom Edge**, or **rest** (short-rest benefits).

**What the bridge has:**
- Loose planks: any span can be unpinned to drop under weight
- Pier-house under the south end: pitch, rope, planks, block and tackle
- Pier ledges just above the water: room for one Medium creature to hide, or climb up behind
- The mill: grain hoist, and a sluice that speeds the current under the south spans
- The gate: timber on iron hinges (built for sheep)
- Chest-high reeds on both banks

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Custom Edge yardstick</b></summary>

| | Tier 1 (10–16) | Tier 2 (17+) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Damage | 2d6 once, or 1d6 once per wave | 3d6 once, or 2d6 once per wave |
| Condition | One creature, **save DC 12** | Up to three, **DC 13**, or one with no save |
| Terrain | One span difficult terrain | One span dropped or blocked for a whole wave |

</details>

## 5–7. The fight for the bridge

> [!WARNING]
> **The Burn track (every wave).** At the end of each round, if an enemy is on the **south landing** and no conscious defender is within **10 ft**, tick a box.
> **Clear a box:** an action + **Athletics DC 12**, or drive every enemy off the landing. **Three boxes = the bridge is lost.**
> Fire on the deck *north* of the landing doesn't count.

<details>
<summary><b>Between waves</b></summary>

- No rest. Each PC gets **one free thing**: potion, stabilise, move, pass an item.
- Anyone in the river: swept **20 ft** downstream per round; **Athletics DC 12** to grab a pier.

</details>

### The Cinders (all three waves)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Spell save DC 15.** They **deal no damage, ever.** They stand 60–90 ft back, in bow range. They spend big spells when it counts and leave when bloodied or beaten. **They can be caught:** silence, a gag, incapacitated or counterspelled = no escape. A captured Cinder has **the job letter copy** and says *"The Heir doesn't want your bridge. He wants to see how long it takes Brenna to come."*

<details>
<summary><b>Cinder stats</b> (both)</summary>

**AC 13 · HP 52 · Speed 30 · Spell save DC 15** · Wis +4, Cha +7 · darkvision 60 · passive Perception 11

- **Scatter to Ash:** always keeps a *dimension door* (verbal only) for escaping
- **One flame at a time:** one concentration spell each; damage forces a save (DC 10 or half the damage)

| | The Hush | The Elder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At will | *message*, *minor illusion* | *message*, *minor illusion*, **Ash Grip** (10-ft square within 90 ft is difficult terrain until its next turn) |
| 2/day | *silence*, *dimension door* | *dimension door* |
| 1/day | *hold person* (**save it for the duel**) | *confusion*, *transmute rock* |
| Special | **Stilling Word** (Recharge 5–6): one creature within 60 ft, **DC 15 Wis** or can't speak until the end of its next turn | — |

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Suggested spell plan</b></summary>

| Wave | The Hush | The Elder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | *Silence* on the casters/healer when they cluster | *Transmute rock* on the stone landing → **mud for the rest of the fight** |
| 2 | Second *silence*, or Stilling Word | *Confusion* (DC 15 Wis) on the line holding the ram |
| 3 | Hold *hold person* for the duel | Ash Grip; leaves early if it goes badly |

- **Mud counterplay:** fight forward on the deck, or lay planks (an action per 10-ft square).
- **Confusion:** PCs may hit each other. Don't let orcs gang up on a confused PC.

</details>

### Wave 1: The Rush (30 min)

<details>
<summary><b>6 orcs + both Cinders</b></summary>

- **Orc:** AC 13, HP 15, greataxe +5 (1d12+3), Aggressive (SRD)
- Barricade → they spend round 1 breaking it (defenders have half cover). Best moment for the **Pitch Trap**.
- Scouts were spotted → advantage on their first attack.
- **Morale:** 4 orcs down → the rest fall back. The Cinders stay.

</details>

### Wave 2: The Ram (25 min)

<details>
<summary><b>Ogre with ram + 3 escort orcs + 2 javelin orcs + the Cinders</b></summary>

- **Young giant (ogre):** AC 11, HP 59, greatclub +6 (2d8+4) (SRD)
- **Ram:** no attack roll, **2d8+4** to the barricade → then the **gate (AC 15, 30 HP)**
- **Javelin orcs** (north bank): +5, 1d6+3, range 30/120
- Pier-ledge ambush from Watch 2? Reward it.
- **Morale:** giant drops → escorts pull back.

</details>

> [!NOTE]
> **The dawn loaf (Edda's moment).** She limps down the line tearing warm bread from the mill. *"Nobody fights the third one hungry."* Each PC who eats **spends one Hit Die free**. Then: **one horn.**

### Wave 3: The Champion (35 min)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> *"Your warriors have answered mine. Now I answer you."* **Clan law:** Varn and his Shield-kin **won't strike anyone who is down, has yielded, or is paralysed or magically restrained.** Tell the players the first time it matters.

<details>
<summary><b>Varn, son of Ushra</b> · AC 16 · HP 90</summary>

**Str +4 · Dex +1 · Con +3** · saves Str +6, Con +5 · Athletics +6 · darkvision 60 · CR 4

- **Multiattack:** two greataxe attacks, **+6, 1d12+4** (10)
- **Aggressive:** bonus action, move up to 30 ft toward a hostile creature
- **Relentless Endurance (1/day):** drops to 1 HP instead of 0
- **Parry (reaction):** +2 AC against one melee attack
- **Champion's Challenge:** the first time he **starts his turn at 45 HP or less** → see below

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Shield-kin ×2</b> · AC 17 · HP 38</summary>

Athletics +5 · passive Perception 13 · CR 1

- **Spear:** +5, 1d6+3 (reach 5 or thrown 20/60)
- **Shield Shove (bonus action):** **DC 13 Str** or pushed 5 ft / knocked prone. Off the deck edge = **into the river**.
- **Ward (reaction):** disadvantage on an attack against Varn from anyone within 5 ft of the guard

</details>

> [!WARNING]
> **Champion's Challenge at 45 HP or less.** Varn plants his spear and points at whoever has **hurt him most** (check the tally; if unsure, whoever took him under 45). *"If I fall, we go home. If you fall, the river is ours and the bridge burns."*

<details>
<summary><b>Duel rules (clan law)</b></summary>

- **One champion each.** The challenged PC may name a stand-in → Varn: *"You hid behind them."*
- **The champion's breath:** the challenged PC may spend **up to half their Hit Dice**. Varn doesn't.
- **One second each:** can heal, shield and shout, but **can't strike**.
- **Everyone else steps back.** Shield-kin lower their shields. **The Burn track stops.**
- Ends when a champion **yields or falls**. The winner decides the loser's life.
- **Party refuses the challenge:** the fight goes on; at 1 HP (Relentless Endurance) Varn yields. Choice II still happens.
- **Fragile caster won't name a stand-in?** Start Varn at 35 HP.

</details>

> [!CAUTION]
> **The interference (round 2 of the duel, or when Varn drops below 20).** The Hush casts ***hold person* (DC 15 Wis)** on the champion from the fog.
> **Spotting it:** passive Perception 13+, or anyone watching the Cinders. They may act once: **warn** (advantage on the save), **shoot/throw** (forces a concentration save as the spell lands), or **charge**.
> **Champion held →** Varn stops his axe mid-swing, roars, the Shield-kin go for the Hush (she Scatters to Ash). *"There's no honour left on this bridge today. I yield."* **Heroes win.**
> Hush captured, dead or out of *hold person*? **Skip it.**

<details>
<summary><b>Other endings</b></summary>

- **Champion loses:** Varn lowers his axe. *"You held long enough."* The bridgehead burns, the village is untouched → **Burned by the warband**.
- **Burn track hits 3:** the warband pulls back north ahead of the fire. Varn raises his spear and goes west → **Burned by the warband**.
- **Players burn it themselves:** Edda is furious but won't fight them. At dawn Varn throws them **his cord** across the river: *"Ask your duchess what Corwen signed at this river."* **No Choice II.** The Cinders lead **6 hotheads** across the ford to fire the mill (run it like Wave 1).
- **A wave goes badly** (2 boxes, party on the ropes): Varn walks on early and calls the challenge.

</details>

## 8. Choice II: Varn's life (10 min)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Edda: "Do it." The militia: "Do it." Fenn looks at the river. Varn: *"My life is yours. Take it or give it back."*

<details>
<summary><b>Spared</b></summary>

- Gives the champion **his cord** (half a broken thorn-and-crown seal; thread 1).
- *"My mother keeps the other half. Ask your duchess what Corwen signed at this river. Then ask her why she still keeps the pen."* (He won't name the Thorn Pact.)
- *"The grey ones carried us around your fence. My mother paid their fare with this raid. Tell her the debt is paid."* (thread 2)
- No Cinder captured? He hands over **the job letter copy** from the Elder's pallet: *"They knew you were coming before you did."* (thread 3)
- *"When you need the clans, send the cord north."*
- → **Ushra stays out of the Host** (for now).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Killed</b></summary>

- The militia cheers; Edda notches her stick. The orcs carry his body west in silence.
- The cord goes with him, and the Thorn Pact hint is lost for now.
- → **Ushra joins the Host** (Act III).

</details>
