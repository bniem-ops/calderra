# Hold the Bridge at Harrow's Ford

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- A panicked village → a night of preparation (barricades, traps, training villagers; each success buys an edge) → three escalating waves → the warband's champion demands single combat.

## The choice

Spare or kill the defeated champion. Sparing him creates a future orc ally against the Ashen Pretender.

## Ripples

- Duchess Ironhart takes notice of Lantern-sworn heroes.
- The refugees resettle in Greenhollow.

## DM notes

Your Gauntlet-style skill challenge turned into battle prep, then a big heroic fight. The players feel clever before they feel strong.
