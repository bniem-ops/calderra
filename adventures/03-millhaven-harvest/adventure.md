# The Millhaven Harvest

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- MAX asks for help → negotiate with both quartermasters → stop a sabotage plot meant to start a battle → defend the granary, or make both armies stand down.

## The choice

Which army, if either, leaves with grain.

## Ripples

- Whichever army leaves with grain gets stronger for Act II.

## DM notes

A light callback to the Festival. Anyone who played it gets a wink from MAX; anyone who didn't just meets a great innkeeper.
