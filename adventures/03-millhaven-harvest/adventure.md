> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- MAX asks for help → negotiate with both quartermasters → stop a sabotage plot meant to start a battle → defend the granary, or make both armies stand down.

## The Mind gem

<!-- secret -->
- The **Mind gem** is tangled in the dead roots of the Festival's Challenger, under the Millhaven coliseum. It's leaking: the village children have all started having the same dream.
- One of the armies (or a Temple agent with it) has heard a gem is here. "Foraging" is cover for digging.
- The heroes can dig it out first. Whoever carries it afterwards starts seeing visions (its cost when alone).
- It ends the session with an NPC, not a PC: ideally Ser Maren and the Order of the Dawn Lantern. If the heroes fail, whoever dug it up keeps it.
- See `world/sunfire-crown.md`.

## The news from the Ford (Act I's closing beat)

<!-- secret -->
The players know the headlines from the map: the Ford burned, and a Regency company died first. This is where they get the story from someone who was there. The full aftermath is in `ledger/sessions/01-harrows-ford.md`.

**Edda is here.** The Millhaven carter brought her down the back lanes with a column of Ford-folk. She washes pots at the Broken Antler for MAX and keeps her tally stick on the bar. It has new notches.

**When:** after the harvest is settled, at the Broken Antler that night. Don't open with it; it should land as the last thing Act I says. If no Harrow's Ford hero is at the table, it still works: she's a refugee telling strangers why she's a refugee.

**What she tells them, in her own order** (she swears a lot, and she isn't good at speeches):
1. *"Your orc kept his word. I'll give him that. Barricaded his end and sat there."*
2. A rider went south to the Regent, *"not the duchess, mind,"* and the Regent sent a captain and a dozen swords to take the north bank back.
3. *"Your orc met them on his barricade and offered them the old fight. One against one. Captain said no. They all said no."* None of them came back. The sun banner came down the river past the mill.
4. *"Then he crossed."* The village, the mill and the bridge burned, from the south end so it can't be rebuilt. He let anyone who ran or knelt go.
5. She puts the tally stick on the table. She doesn't say whose notches they are.

**How she feels about the heroes:** decide before the session. She can blame them (*"You promised him something that wasn't yours to give"*), thank them (*"You bought us a month. The Regent spent it"*), or both in the same breath. Both is the most Edda.

**If they ask about Varn:** *"Went west along the river with the smoke. Took his cord with him."*

**Act I's last line:** *the peace you made lasted until someone else broke it.*
