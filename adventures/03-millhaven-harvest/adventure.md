# The Millhaven Harvest

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- MAX asks for help → negotiate with both quartermasters → stop a sabotage plot meant to start a battle → defend the granary, or make both armies stand down.

## The Mind gem

<!-- secret -->
- The **Mind gem** is tangled in the dead roots of the Festival's Challenger, under the Millhaven coliseum. It's leaking: the village children have all started having the same dream.
- One of the armies (or a Temple agent with it) has heard a gem is here. "Foraging" is cover for digging.
- The heroes can dig it out first. Whoever carries it afterwards starts seeing visions (its cost when alone).
- It ends the session with a faction, not a PC: ideally the Order of the Dawn Lantern. If the heroes fail, whoever dug it up keeps it.
- See `world/sunfire-crown.md`.
<!-- /secret -->

## The choice

Which army, if either, leaves with grain, and who leaves with the gem.

## Ripples

- Whichever army leaves with grain gets stronger for Act II.
- Whoever holds the Mind gem: update `ledger/state.yaml`. Every claimant hears about it before the Feast of Banners.

## DM notes

A light callback to the Festival. Anyone who played it gets a wink from MAX; anyone who didn't just meets a great innkeeper.

## Pretender link

<!-- secret -->
The Host has been told to spare Millhaven: MAX is the Heir's uncle. If the Host's foragers are one of the threats, they'll back off when they learn whose inn this is. A good place for heroes to find MAX's sister's lockbox, if they still have his key. See `world/factions/ashen-pretender.md`.
