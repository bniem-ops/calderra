# The Regent's Vault

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- Plan the heist (let the players draw it) → infiltrate a palace gala → the vault's puzzle locks (the Seven Chests cipher, full-session version) → escape when it goes wrong.

## What's in the vault

The Heart gem, and the Regent's archive: the Last Sun's sickbed records, the Quiet Seal, the ledger of dragon tribute paid to the dwarves and the hush money paid west to the exiles, and the Last Sun's written acknowledgement of his son (the Ashen Pretender). See `world/factions/regency.md`.

## The choice

Take the gem, or leave it once they learn the Regent is keeping it safe. (The Heart binds Lysander to his promise to keep the empire whole; taking it frees him of it, and he may not thank them.) And: **what else do they take, and who do they show it to?**

## DM notes

At 7th level expect invisibility, passwall and fly. Design wards that answer those spells instead of blocking them.

## Harrow's Ford echoes

*Optional. Drop one if it fits; none of them changes the session.*

- **In the archive:** the Quill copy of **Captain Aurel Dane's writ** ("retake the north bank"). Beside it is the company's pay booked against *the March account*. It's the wall-pay trick in reverse, and a good small proof of how Lysander's books work.
- **Out of the palace windows:** the Ford-folk refugee camp outside the north gate. It's a good place to stage the escape, or to lose pursuers.
