# The Forging

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

<!-- secret -->
## Premise

All three gems are needed to forge a new Sunfire Crown, in dragonfire, at the forge-heart of Mount Kharag, by a Holdfast master smith. This one-shot is a heist in reverse: get the gems, the smith and the heroes *in*, and hold the forge for one night while everyone tries to stop or steal the crown. See `world/sunfire-crown.md`.

## What has to be true going in (check the ledger)

- **The forge is lit:** the young dragon was bargained with in The Dragon of Mount Kharag. If it was killed, the forge is cold: the heroes need another fire (a much harder quest), or the Forging can't happen and the Council goes ahead without a crown.
- **The smith:** Torvek Nameless, if the heroes won him over. Otherwise Hilde Stonecrown (good, not great: the crown may come out flawed).
- **The gems:** who holds each one. Any gem not in the heroes' allies' hands has to be won, bought or taken tonight.
  - **Will gem:** stolen → it's here, and the Host is coming for it. Turned → Rowan brings it himself, as an ally. Left → he comes with it, and an army, to take the crown.

## Beats

1. **The road in.** Escort the smith and the gems up the mountain past the Host, Temple agents and Hargrove's hired blades.
2. **The bargain at the forge.** The young dragon must breathe on the forge. Whatever was promised at Kharag comes due.
3. **The night of the forging.** A siege in stages while Torvek works: each hour a new faction tries to break in, bargain, or steal a gem. Track the forging with a progress clock.
4. **The crown.** Whoever is holding it when the forge cools has the strongest claim at the Council.

## The choice

Who carries the new crown down the mountain to Solace, and what they promise to do with it.

## Ripples

- Set in a crown together, the gems stop costing their bearers: Rowan's burn stops; Lysander is freed of his promise.
- A whole crown starts closing the cracks, whether or not anyone wears it.
- Whoever carries the crown to the Council gets +2 standing with the faction that backed them, and −1 with everyone else.
