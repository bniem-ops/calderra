# The Pretender's Mask

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- Disguise and infiltration (illusions, forged orders, stolen uniforms) → camp politics among monster clans → see the Pretender unmasked → escape with the truth and, if lucky, the Will gem.

## Twist

- The Pretender really is of the imperial bloodline.
- Under the mask: grey ash spreading from his chest (the Will gem is burning him) and his father's face. See `world/factions/ashen-pretender.md`.
- Watch the Hearth: the one loaf, the names spoken, the Heir carrying water. The heroes should leave unsure who the villain is.

## The Will gem

<!-- secret -->
Three ways out, each shaping The Forging (see `world/sunfire-crown.md`):
1. **Steal it.** Very hard. The Host loses its iron will and starts to splinter; the burn stops; Rowan survives, weaker and furious.
2. **Turn him.** Show him the lockbox, his uncle MAX and the acknowledgement, and tell him a new crown is the only thing that stops the burn. He brings the Will gem to the Forging as an ally.
3. **Leave it.** He comes to the Forging with the gem and an army, to take the crown for himself.
<!-- /secret -->

## The choice

Depose him, turn him, or take the gem.

## Ripples

- Learning he's a true heir reshapes the finale.

## DM notes

At 11th level the heroes can teleport and scry. Give the camp anti-scrying wards and a giant warlord or two.
