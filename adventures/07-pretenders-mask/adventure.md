# The Pretender's Mask

> Everything below the header is DM-only. Only the header fields above appear on the player map (the cold open only once its act is current).

## Beats

- Disguise and infiltration (illusions, forged orders, stolen uniforms) → camp politics among monster clans → see the Pretender unmasked → escape with the truth and, if lucky, the Will gem.

## Twist

- The Pretender really is of the imperial bloodline.

## The choice

Depose him, or turn him.

## Ripples

- Learning he's a true heir reshapes the finale.

## DM notes

At 11th level the heroes can teleport and scry. Give the camp anti-scrying wards and a giant warlord or two.
