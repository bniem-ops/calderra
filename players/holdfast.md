This is what your character knows growing up in the mountains. It's your people's story as *they* tell it. Not everything a people believes about itself is true.

## Who you are

You are **Holdfast**: one of the dwarves of the Ironspine, which your people simply call **the Spine**. Lowlanders call your nation "the Stonecrown Holds" after the ruling clan, and call you **Spiners** when they think you can't hear.

Your people were not *from* anywhere. The Spine dreamed, and its dream was the dwarves. The First Thane woke inside the mountain with a hammer already in her hand, and her seven sons founded the seven clans. Calderra grew up around you; you were here first, and you'll be here last.

What defines you: **you keep your word.** Lowlanders say you keep *score*. They're not entirely wrong.

## Your home

- **The holds** are cities carved deep into the mountain, one for each clan. The oldest, **the Great Hold**, holds the Moot hall, the Hall of Names and the great forges, whose fires have burned for a thousand years on the volcano's heat.
- **There is no sun below,** so life runs on **bell-shifts**: three eight-hour turns rung by the great bell.
- The Spine gives iron, silver, stone, heat and clean water. It gives no bread. **Every loaf comes up the passes from Greenhollow** each autumn, bought with metal. Dwarves don't like to talk about that.
- Far below the Great Hold is a door that has always been sealed. Nobody opens it, nobody talks about it at dinner, and children are told not to whistle in the mines because of it.

## How you're governed

- You belong to a **clan**, a family line from one of the First Thane's sons. Five clans survive. Two died out, and their stone chairs in the Moot are turned to face the wall.
- **The Moot** is the council: one seat per clan. It elects **the Thane** for life.
- The current Thane is **Durgan Stonecrown**: grumpy, honourable, fond of a good drink. Clan Stonecrown has held the thaneship for five generations, which everyone agrees is a coincidence.
- Durgan's niece **Hilde Stonecrown** is talked about as the next Thane. She wants the Holds to trade openly with the lowlands and send a voice to the Council of Banners. The old guard hates the idea.
- **Breaking a contract** means paying it back double, in front of the Moot. **Breaking an oath** means your beard is shorn, your name is chiselled off the Ledger Stone, and you join **the Oathless**, who work the deepest, hottest seams for bread and a bunk.

## What you hold sacred

- **A sworn oath.** Even to a lowlander. *Especially* to a lowlander, because one dwarf's broken word makes every dwarf a liar.
- **Your name-blade,** forged when you came of age. You would sooner sell your hand.
- **The Hall of Names.** When an honoured dwarf dies, the eldest carver cuts their name into its walls, and the mountain holds them forever. A dwarf who dies uncarved fades into the rock. Being remembered correctly matters more to you than gold.
- **The forge-fire.** Letting a forge go cold through neglect is a disgrace.

**Your virtue:** endurance. Keep going, keep your word, keep the forge lit.
**Your vice:** the grudge. The Holds keep a **Grudge Ledger** beside the Hall of Names, and it's longer than the treaties. You'd call it "a long memory."

## Life, food and ceremony

- **A normal day:** black bread and mushroom porridge, a shift at the forge, the mine or the ledger-house, then the clan hall for ale, songs that are mostly lists of names, and arguments about who owes whom.
- **A feast:** forge-bread baked right on the anvil stones, goat roasted over volcanic vents, black mushroom stew, juniper-smoked cave-fish, and **Kharag Dark**, a stout so thick it's poured with a spoon. Apples on the table mean someone spent a fortune.
- **A wedding:** both names carved together on one stone; each partner swears an oath to the other's clan; then together they forge a single ring, one striking and one holding.
- **A funeral:** the body is laid in the deep stone, the clan gathers while the name is carved, and the last line of the funeral song is the name itself, sung once by everyone.

## How you talk

**Sayings**
- "Stone remembers." (A promise, a threat or a blessing, depending on tone.)
- "Measure twice, swear once."
- "Bread is borrowed; iron is owed."

**Insults**
- "Paper-promise": anyone whose word is worth no more than the parchment.
- "May your name wear smooth."
- "Soft-handed."

**Superstitions**
- Never whistle below ground.
- A sputtering forge means someone in the clan has told a lie.
- Don't speak the old dragon's name inside the mountain. Nobody remembers who started that one.

## What you think of everyone else

- **The old empire / the Regency:** centuries of imperial tax, and what did the Holds get? Roads you didn't need and soldiers you never saw. Good riddance.
- **House Ironhart:** the Thornwall is built of *your* stone, and the March never fully paid for it. And they claim the high pass. Still, Duchess Brenna fights her own battles. You respect that, grudgingly.
- **Greenhollow:** polite farmers who grow the bread you can't. You're careful to be courteous to them, and careful not to need them out loud.
- **The Saltreach Compact:** the only lowlanders who keep honest books. Your best customers.
- **The dragon of Mount Kharag:** for a hundred years the empire paid it to sleep. Now there's smoke over the mountain, and no one in the Holds will say much about it.
- **The orc and giant clans:** every raid on the northern passes is written in the Grudge Ledger, by name.

## Playing a Holdfast character

**Character ideas**
- **A young Daylighter** who thinks the Holds must open up or die, and has come down to the lowlands to prove it.
- **An Oathless dwarf** out to earn their name back with one great deed. (The Moot has restored names before. Rarely.)
- **A caravan guard** who has walked the Greenhollow grain road every autumn of their life and knows every inn on it.
- **A ledger-keeper** sent to collect a very old debt from someone in the lowlands.
- **A Hall of Names carver** who believes a hero's name is waiting to be cut, and wants to be there when it happens.

**Rules fit (no new rules needed)**
- **Species:** dwarf.
- **Backgrounds that fit:** Guild Artisan (smith, mason, brewer), Soldier (pass warden), Guard, Outlander (caravans), Acolyte (keeper of the Hall of Names), Merchant.
- **Classes that fit well:** Fighter, Paladin (oath-keepers), Cleric (forge or knowledge), Barbarian, Rogue (a ledger-keeper who knows where every coin went), Artificer if your table allows it.

**Naming**
- Given name, then clan name: *Thyra Stonecrown*.
- Four of the five living clans haven't been named yet. **Pick one and name it,** then tell your DM. It becomes canon.
- An Oathless dwarf has no clan name. They go by their given name and "Nameless": *Kelda Nameless*.
- More given names: Orsa, Dagny, Rurik, Inga, Holm, Sigrun, Vali, Brokk, Torvald, Hekla.

**Not a dwarf?** Lowlanders work the caravans and the surface terraces, but they're never invited inside. A halfling or human raised on the grain road can still use most of this guide, as an outsider who knows the Holdfast better than most.
