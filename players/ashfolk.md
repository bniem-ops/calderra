This is what your character knows growing up in the burnt far west, or after joining the Host. It's the story as *they* tell it. You can play someone still loyal to the Host, someone who left it, or someone from the frontier who never joined.

## Who you are

You are **Ashfolk**, from **the Ashen Frontier**: the far west of Calderra, burned in the empire's first wars and never rebuilt. Or you're one of the **Branded**, someone who ran from the rest of Calderra and found a place here. Everyone else calls the frontier a wasteland. You call it home, because nobody else wanted it.

The empire throws people away: the orc clans it walled out, the sailors it sold into bonds, the soldiers it branded for running, the farmers it starved. The frontier is where the thrown-away end up. For a hundred years nobody came here but outlaws and ghosts.

Then the Heir came. Now the thrown-away have an army, and a claim to the throne.

What defines you: **nothing is wasted.** Not a person, not a coin, not a grudge. Outsiders say you burn everything you touch.

## Your home

- **The ruined forts** of the old imperial wars are where most Ashfolk live: crumbling stone, good walls if you don't mind the ghosts, and old battlefields nearby full of rusted weapons and better ones buried deeper.
- The land is hard. Little grows, and the rivers run grey. Families keep goats, bake **ash-bread** in the coals, and trade with whoever comes.
- The frontier is mixed: orc and goblin families whose ancestors fled west long ago, half-orcs, humans who ran from somewhere, a few dwarves without clan names, the odd ogre who prefers quiet.
- Since the emperor died, the frontier has become the camp of **the Ashen Host**.

## The Heir and the Host

- **The Heir** is what the Host calls him. Everyone else calls him the **Ashen Pretender**. He's young, masked, and claims to be the Last Sun's son, thrown away by the empire.
- He grew up in the frontier. He learned the clans' tongues, ate at their fires and dug their wells. When the emperor died, he spent everything he had on food for the frontier first, and weapons second. That's why people follow him.
- **The Ashen Host** is his army: the outcast clans, hired ogre companies, **the Branded** (deserters, runaways, clanless dwarves), and anyone else the empire threw away.
- **Kestrel**, his herald, speaks for him in public. Nobody's quite sure where Kestrel came from.
- **The Hearth** is the war council. Every night the Heir and the chiefs sit at one fire and tear **one loaf** of bread between them. A chief who won't eat from the loaf is no longer at the Hearth.
- **The banner** is the imperial sun turned upside down: the empire's sun has set, and the Host is what's left.

## The Host's rules

- **Nothing is wasted.** Deserters aren't executed. They're sent to the front line of the next battle.
- **Steal from the stores** and you go without for as many days as you stole.
- **Harm a surrendered enemy** and you're put out of the Host, unarmed, at dusk. The Heir is very strict about this.
- **No walls.** The Host camps in the open, in tents, even next to a ruined fort. Walls are what were used to keep you out.
- **Promotion is by deeds,** and fast. Nobody stays at the bottom long.

## What you hold sacred

- **The loaf.** Bread torn by hand and shared at one fire. Breaking bread with someone is a vow.
- **Names.** Every member's name is spoken at the Hearth the night they join and the night they die. The empire forgot you. The Host doesn't. As long as your name is spoken at the fire, you are still in the Host, even after death.
- **Each clan's own gods.** The Host has no single faith, and nobody is asked to give theirs up.

**Your virtue:** loyalty, to whoever shares your bread.
**Your vice:** burning. "They taught us hunger. Now we teach it back."

## Life, food and ceremony

- **A normal day in the Host:** drums at dawn; drill, forage or march; raiding parties out and supply wagons in. At dusk every fire tears its own loaf and speaks the names of the day's dead.
- **A feast:** whatever was taken from the enemy, shared out equally, and always ash-bread, flat and black from the coals, eaten first by the youngest.
- **A wedding:** the couple tears one loaf between them in front of the fire, and each eats the other's half.
- **A funeral:** the name is spoken, the body is burned, and the ash is rubbed on the faces of the dead's company before their next battle.

## How you talk

**Sayings**
- "Nothing is wasted."
- "Who threw you away?" (How you greet a new recruit.)
- "They taught us hunger."

**Insults**
- "Fence-builder": anyone who walls others out.
- "Granary-fat."
- "Sun-blind": anyone who still believes in the empire.

**Superstitions**
- Never sleep inside walls if you can help it.
- If your name is forgotten at the Hearth, you'll die in the next fight. Say your own name twice before battle, to be sure.
- Never look under the Heir's mask.

## What you think of everyone else

- **The Regency:** the liars at the centre of everything. They sell bread back to the people who grew it, and they threw away their own emperor's son.
- **House Ironhart:** the fence-builders. Half the Host's grandparents were walled out of the March.
- **The dwarves of the Ironspine:** rich, proud, and unable to feed themselves. Some of the Host's best smiths are dwarves the Holds threw away.
- **The Saltreach Compact:** merchants who'll sell to anyone. Useful.
- **Greenhollow:** farmers, mostly blameless, on the road the war has to pass through.
- **The Order of the Dawn Lantern:** the only enemy the Heir calls honest.

## Playing an Ashfolk character

**Character ideas**
- **A Host soldier who walked away** after a burning they couldn't stomach, and is now looking for some other way to fix things.
- **A frontier scavenger** who knows every ruined fort and old battlefield in the west, and sells what they dig up.
- **One of the Branded:** a Thornwall deserter with the thorn on their hand, a runaway bond-sailor, or a clanless dwarf, trying to start again.
- **An orc or goblin from an outcast clan** sent east by their chief to see the world the Host is fighting.
- **A true believer** in the Heir who has joined the heroes to watch them, or to show them the other side.

**Rules fit (no new rules needed)**
- **Species:** any, but especially orc, half-orc, goblin, human, and dwarf (clanless).
- **Backgrounds that fit:** Outlander, Soldier, Criminal, Folk Hero, Hermit, Urchin, Wayfarer.
- **Classes that fit well:** Barbarian, Ranger, Fighter, Rogue, Druid (the burnt land), Cleric (a clan's god), Warlock.

**Naming**
- Clan members use clan names: *Durra of the Red Hand*, *Skell Ashtooth*.
- The Branded often take a new name when they join the Host, and only say the old one at the Hearth.
- More given names: Garr, Mav, Yarra, Teller, Brun, Ishka, Dov, Rook, Senna, Tamm.

**Talk to your DM first.** Playing someone with ties to the Host is a great way to bring the war's other side to the table, and your DM may have a secret or two to give you.
