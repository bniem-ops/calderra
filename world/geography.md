# Calderra's regions: how they fit together

> DM-only. The seven region files in `world/regions/` each cover one place. This file is the connective tissue: the travel network, the threads that run through every region, and the contrasts that keep each one distinct. Check here before adding a new place, so it plays with the rest.

## The shape of the continent

Calderra is one landmass. Reading the continent map (`map/assets/calderra.png`) from west to east:

- **The Ashen Frontier**, the burned grey west, on the western coast.
- **Greenhollow**, the hedged farmland west of centre.
- **The Heartlands**, the middle, with Solace on the **Sunwater**, the great river that flows south-east from the capital to the sea.
- **The Gilded Delta**, where the Sunwater breaks into seven mouths in the south-east.
- **The Saltreach Coast**, the eastern seaboard between the Delta and the mountains.
- **The Ironspine** in the north-east, with Mount Kharag smoking at its far end.
- **The March of Thorns**, the cold strip along the north. The Thornwall runs along its northern edge, from the sea in the west to the foot of the Ironspine in the east. The clan lands lie beyond it.

**The map isn't to scale.** It's a painted map, and the Lanternhold looks farther from Solace than it is. Use the travel table below, not the pin positions.

All times are by cart or on foot. A rider is about a third faster. A road through a war zone is as slow as the DM likes.

| Solace | Vareth (the Delta) | 4 days downstream by barge, 6 back up; 7 by road | The Sunwater |
| Solace | Highwater (Saltreach) | 6 days by road | East road |

**What this means at the table:** Solace is the hub, and every route between two regions either passes through it or pays someone to avoid it. Greenhollow feeds the Holds by the pass road. Saltreach reaches Solace by the river, so it pays Vareth's toll. The Delta sits on the capital's only way to the sea.

## Shared threads

Things every region has, each in its own way. Use them to make a new place feel part of the same world.

### The empire's leftovers: road, inn and sun-mark

The empire built the same things everywhere: paved roads with milestones, a coaching inn a day's ride apart on every main road, and a **sun-mark** (the imperial sun emblem) on every public building. What each region does with them now shows where it stands:

| Region | The road | The sun-marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heartlands | Every road ends here; safe within a day of Solace, lawless beyond | Smashed at night, by three different groups |
| Greenhollow | The grain road burns at its western end | Left alone. A harvest knot is hung over them at the Festival, as it always was. |
| March of Thorns | The north road ends at the wall | Kept and scrubbed. The wall was sworn to the Crown, and Marchers keep their posts. |
| Saltreach | The sea is the road; the east road is for ore and wool | Chiselled off the week the Compact was signed and replaced with the Compact's knot |
| Ironspine | The pass road stops at the Doorstep; lowlanders go no further | There never were any inside the holds. The dwarves never allowed them. |
| Ashen Frontier | Broken imperial roads to ruined forts, going nowhere | Turned upside down on every fort: the Host's setting sun |
| Gilded Delta | No roads worth the name; canals and barges | Prised off and sold for the gilt. You can buy one in the Vareth market. |

### Bread

Every region's politics comes back to bread. Greenhollow grows it. Solace rations it through the Granaries. The Holds and the March buy it. The Reach buys it and carries it. The Host burns it and shares it from one loaf. The Delta grows the other half of Calderra's grain and sells it to whoever pays most. When you're stuck for a scene, ask who in it is hungry and who is selling.

### The lamp in the window

Since the Festival of the Accord, people hang a lamp for the Lantern-sworn. How far the custom has spread depends on the ledger. It's strong in Greenhollow; in the March it depends on what happened at Harrow's Ford; in the Reach, captains hang one at the masthead for a ship the Order helped; dwarves think it's a lowland fad; the Host uses the same lamp oil; in Vareth, a silver lamp in a window is this season's fashion and means nothing.

### Names and the dead

Every people in Calderra fears being forgotten, and each has its own way of remembering:
- **Greenhollow:** buried under the hedge.
- **Solace:** written in the Book of Light.
- **The March:** ashes mortared into the wall.
- **The Reach:** painted on the ship's stern.
- **The Holds:** carved in the Hall of Names.
- **The Host:** spoken at the Hearth.
- **Vareth:** read out from the ledger at the funeral, with every debt and credit passed to the heirs.

It's a thread to pull at the Council of Banners and in the Legacy Heroes list.

### The Host's shadow

The Host turns up in every region, never in the same shape twice:
- **Greenhollow:** raiders burning the grain road.
- **The March:** boats around the wall.
- **Solace:** sun-mark smashers.
- **The Reach:** crates of "grain" bound west.
- **The Holds:** an offer to the Oathless.
- **The Delta:** Host coin at the mercenary auctions, from a buyer nobody can name.

This feeds the running thread from `dawn-lantern.md`: in every one-shot, one Ashen presence that shouldn't be there.

### The hollow count

Every region has a gap between what its books say and what's on the ground. It's the same story told seven times: the empire ran on promises written down, and with the Crown gone, people are starting to count.

| Region | The books say | The ground says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greenhollow | The Long Barns are half full | A quarter. Factor Pell sold the rest. |
| Heartlands | The city granaries are half full | A quarter. Lysander knows. |
| March of Thorns | Twelve manned towers | Nine. Three are hollow, held by straw wardens. |
| Ironspine | Forty years of tribute delivered to a dragon | Forged seals; the old dragon long dead |
| Ashen Frontier | A frontier garrison, paid every year as wall pay | No garrison. The hush money went to an exiled woman and her son. |
| Saltreach | Crates of grain for Greenhollow | Crossbows for the Host |
| Gilded Delta | Vareth's vaults hold the gold behind every banknote | They hold about a third. The rest is promises. |

Heroes who find one hollow count can learn to look for the others. A brain-spotlight one-shot could be built on nothing else.

### The cracks wear local faces

The broken Crown's cracks (see `sunfire-crown.md`) stay in the background until late. When they start leaking, they show up as each region's own folklore getting slightly worse. Players will put it down to superstition for a long time. Drop one of these per session at most, from Act II on:

| Region | The old superstition | What it starts doing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greenhollow | Never thank a stranger in the Hollow | Fey mischief in parishes that never had an Accord; the children's shared dream |
| Heartlands | If the temple flame flickers, someone important dies | The eternal flame leans toward the vault, as if something below were breathing |
| March of Thorns | A blue beacon flame means the dead walk the watch | Blue flames on towers where nobody has died |
| Saltreach | A ghost ship in fog is someone's unpaid debt | Ghost ships nobody's ledger can account for, flying no flag in Calderra |
| Ironspine | Never whistle below ground; it calls something from under the Sealed Door | The forge-fires in the Great Hold lean toward the Door |
| Ashen Frontier | Never sleep inside walls | Embers in century-old ash still warm; the grey rivers run warm |
| Gilded Delta | Never row the eighth mouth | In fog, the Sunwater has eight mouths, and boats that take the eighth come back a day late or not at all |

Only three of these sit on true cracks: Solace (the Heart's), the far west (the Will's) and the Hollow (the Mind's). The Sealed Door is a fourth, older seal (see `stonecrown.md`). The rest are the world thinning everywhere as the seals fail. That's the epilogue if no crown is made.

## What keeps each region distinct

| | Land | Its road | Genre (tone pillar) | What a promise is worth | Light and time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Greenhollow** | Hedges, fields, slow rivers | The road runs through it | Village festival, defence | A handshake: "a bargain kept" | The seasons, the harvest |
| **Heartlands** | Vines, marble, a domed city | Every road ends here | Courtly intrigue, heist, finale | Ink: "if it's not in ink, it's in the wind" | Dawn bells and the Morning Price |
| **March of Thorns** | Frozen grass, pine, the wall | The road ends at the wall | Siege, last stand | Your post: "we hold" | Long winter nights, beacon fires |
| **Saltreach** | Harbours, salt marsh, pine | The sea | Sea chase, swashbuckling | A signed contract, settled in full | The tide bell |
| **Ironspine** | Peaks, forges, a volcano | The pass, closed to outsiders | Dragon hunt, dwarven politics | An oath carved in stone | No sun; bell-shifts |
| **Ashen Frontier** | Ash, ruins, grey rivers | Broken roads to nowhere | War camp, wilderness, infiltration | Bread shared from one loaf | Drums at dawn, the Hearth at dusk |
| **Gilded Delta** | Canals, silk, orange groves, mangrove | Water, and the toll on it | Decadence, duels, auctions | Credit, never settled, always owed | Lamplit nights; duels at dawn |

**Pairs that mirror each other, worth playing against each other:**
- **Saltreach and the Delta.** Both are merchants. The Reach keeps honest books and settles every debt; Vareth never settles anything, because an open debt is a hold on someone.
- **The March and the Holds.** Both are proud and both keep a ledger of grievances. The wall is built from the Holds' stone and the March never paid for it.
- **Greenhollow and the Heartlands.** The farm and the table. One grows the bread, the other sets its price, and neither really sees the other.
- **The Frontier and Solace.** The thrown-away and the people who threw them. The Host shares one loaf; Solace licenses every oven.
