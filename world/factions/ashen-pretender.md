# The Ashen Pretender

Wants: the throne, by force. Leads an army of orcs, ogres and worse. His herald **Kestrel** does the talking. Show his raiders early, his herald mid-way, his face only near the finale.

<!-- secret -->
**Hidden truth:** he really is of the imperial bloodline, a bastard son raised in exile. His claim is real; his methods are monstrous. He carries the Will gem.

**Hidden truth:** about five years ago Regent Lysander exiled him and his mother to the far west, then paid them a yearly stipend in imperial gold to stay silent. His mother saved it all. When the Last Sun died and the payments stopped, he used the hoard to raise his first warband. The Regency funded the start of his war. See `regency.md`. (His mother's fate and how he came to lead monsters are still to be built.)
