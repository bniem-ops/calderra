# The Regency

Wants: keep the empire whole under a council. Fair point: splitting up means a century of war. Flaw: Lysander will lie to anyone to keep the peace.

<!-- secret -->
**Hidden truth:** Regent Lysander has the Heart gem and hid it under the palace to stop anyone taking the throne before the Council.

<!-- secret -->
**Hidden truth:** Lysander knows about the Stonecrown dragon fraud. Years ago, as chancellor, he matched the tribute ledgers against a dwarf-forged seal and realised the old dragon Kharagax was dead and the Holds had been pocketing forty years of imperial gold. He told no one, not even the emperor. He's holding it as leverage: the threat that would force the dwarves to back a council-ruled empire, or to hand over the gold, at the moment it matters most (likely the Council of Banners). If the secret comes out any other way, he loses his best card.
