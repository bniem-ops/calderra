# Order of the Dawn Lantern

**Hook machine.** Ser Maren sends heroes where they're needed, which explains why a fresh team of Lantern-sworn heroes turns up wherever trouble starts.

Wants: the Crown goes to someone worthy. Fair point: sworn to the realm, not any claimant. Flaw: too few knights left to act alone.

MAX served as a Dawn Lantern squire in his youth before going home to run the inn.
