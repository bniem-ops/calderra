# How play works: drop-in heroes, rising levels

Every one-shot stands alone with its own cast, and each is set at a planned level that climbs over the year, so the series feels like a campaign without anyone keeping the same character. Players come and go; the world remembers.

## Why the cast keeps changing

The Order of the Dawn Lantern is too small to fight everywhere, so it recruits **Lantern-sworn** heroes for one job at a time. Each one-shot is a new team on a new mission. The world carries the continuity (the ledger, the factions, the Crown gems), not any single PC.

## Level roadmap

| Act | Theme | One-shots | Levels |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prologue | Before the Age of Banners mattered to anyone | Festival of the Accord | 4 |
| Act I: Banners Rise | The empire's collapse hits ordinary people | Harrow's Ford, The Black Gull, The Millhaven Harvest | 4–5 |
| Act II: The Crown Hunt | Heroes chase the Crown gems through palaces and mountains | The Feast of Banners, The Regent's Vault, The Dragon of Mount Kharag | 6–9 |
| Act III: The Pretender's War | The Ashen Pretender marches on the capital, and a new crown is forged | The Pretender's Mask, The Forging, The Council of Banners | 11–13 |

Run the acts in order so the levels climb; one-shots inside an act can go in any order. A filler one-shot can slot in at the current act's level.

## Building characters

- **New or returning, player's call.** Bring a new PC or re-level one you've played before.
- **Same rules every time:** point buy or standard array, average HP per level, any official class or subclass the table allows.
- **Starting magic items by level** (from a short list the DM prepares):
  - Levels 4–5: one uncommon item
  - Levels 6–9: two uncommon items, or one rare
  - Levels 11–13: one very rare and one uncommon, or two rare
- **Nothing carries over automatically.** Each one-shot is balanced on its own. A player may keep one non-magical keepsake for flavour.
- **Prep tip:** send the level and item list a week ahead. Have two or three pregens ready.

## Keeping standalone sessions connected

- **Two-minute "story so far."** Open each session with the recap from the map.
- **Legacy heroes.** A retired PC can return as an NPC in a later one-shot.
- **No required knowledge.** Every one-shot has a cold open that explains its own stakes.
