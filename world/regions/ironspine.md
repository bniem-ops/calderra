# The Ironspine Mountains

Setting for The Dragon of Mount Kharag and The Forging.

This is a **regional pass**. The Holdfast, the Moot and their secrets are in `factions/stonecrown.md`; the forge-heart and the new crown are in `sunfire-crown.md`; how the Spine fits with the other regions is in `world/geography.md`. This file covers the ground.

## The land

The Ironspine fills Calderra's north-east corner: snow-capped peaks, deep valleys, pine on the lower slopes and bare rock above. Mount Kharag stands at the range's far end, a volcano that has smoked this year for the first time in living memory. Its western end meets the Thornwall at the high pass; its southern foothills run down to the Saltreach coast.

The Spine has two worlds. **Outside** is cold and steep: switchback roads, avalanche slopes, mountain goats and the terraces where hired lowlanders grow what little grows. **Inside** are the five holds: cities carved into the mountain, warm from the volcano's roots, lit by forge-light and lamp, with no sun at all. Most lowlanders only ever see the outside.

**What's changed since the emperor died:**
- **Smoke over Kharag.** The tribute stopped, and the mountain started smoking. Everyone in Calderra can see it on a clear day.
- **The grain caravans are late.** The Greenhollow road is burning, and the autumn bread came up the passes thin and late. The holds are rationing, and nobody below is supposed to know.
- **The passes may close.** The Deepkeepers want the gates shut until the Age of Banners is over. It's argued at the Moot every week.
- **Dwarven factors everywhere.** The Daylighters have sent factors to Solace, Highwater and Vareth, selling iron and steel to anyone with coin or grain.
- **No sun-marks to smash.** The dwarves never let the imperial sun inside a hold. The only suns in the Spine were on the toll-house at the Doorstep and on the tribute seals.

## Places a party passes through

| Place | What it is | What a party finds there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Doorstep** | A lowland market town at the foot of the Spine, where the pass road and the ore road arrive. It's as far as most outsiders get. | Dwarven factors, Greenhollow carters, Reacher ore-buyers, caravan inns, and the gate that leads up into the mountain. |
| **The Gate Stair** | A carved stair and switchback road from the Doorstep up to the first hold's outer gate. | Holdfast gate-wardens who ask your business, your name and who will vouch for you. |
| **The Great Hold** | The oldest hold: the Moot's chamber, the Hall of Names, the Grudge Ledger, and the Sealed Door far beneath. Five generations of Stonecrown thanes have ruled from here. | Thane Durgan, Hilde Stonecrown, carvers at work on new names, and lowlanders admitted only by invitation. |
| **The other four holds** | One per surviving clan, each in its own mountain, linked by deep roads under the peaks. | Clan politics, clan feuds, and a different welcome at each. Name them when a session needs them. |
| **The deep seams** | The hottest, most dangerous mines, below the holds. | The Oathless, working for bread and a bunk; Torvek Nameless, if he's found. |
| **The terraces** | Stepped fields and goat pastures on the outer slopes, worked by hired lowlanders. | Lowland labourers who've worked for the dwarves for years and never been inside. |
| **The tribute road** | The old imperial road from Solace to the foot of Mount Kharag, where the tribute caravans went every year. | Milestones carved with the sun, an empty imperial toll-house, and this year's late caravan. |
| **Mount Kharag** | The volcano at the range's end. The forge-heart is deep inside, the lair is above it, and the feeding tunnel runs between. | Heat, smoke, lava channels, a young dragon, and Kharagax's bones. |

**Roads:** the **pass road** comes up from Greenhollow and the **tribute road** from Solace, both to the Doorstep. The **ore road** goes down to the Saltreach coast. The **high pass** leads west into the March; it's disputed and closed in winter. Inside, the deep roads link the holds. Travel times are in `world/geography.md`.

**Inside or outside** is the Spine's big decision for a DM: a one-shot that stays at the Doorstep and the terraces is a lowland story about dwarves. One that gets invited in is a dwarven story. Getting in should feel like an achievement.

## The Spine in its adventures

- **The Dragon of Mount Kharag (Act II):** escort the late tribute caravan up the tribute road (forged seals in the cargo); hold politics in the Great Hold; then into Kharag by the feeding tunnel or the outer slopes. The battle map is the lair: lava channels, gold and a stone ledge.
- **The Forging (Act III):** every army in Calderra converges on one mountain. The road in runs from the Doorstep, through or around the holds, to the forge-heart. Where the holds stand by then depends on the ledger: a bargained dragon and a kept secret mean the gates open; an exposed fraud means the Spine is sealed and the heroes go in the hard way.

## Secrets of the ground

- **The feeding tunnel.** A hot tunnel runs from the deep seams of the nearest hold to a ledge below the young dragon's lair. The Holdfast sent carts of goat carcasses down it for twenty years. It's the quiet way into Kharag, and it ends at the dwarves' worst secret.
- **The vault and the bones.** The forty years of tribute gold sits in the Stonecrown vaults in the Great Hold. Kharagax's bones lie deeper in the volcano, beyond the young dragon's lair, near the forge-heart. The cold runes on the forge wake when the dragon breathes.
- **The Oathless are being courted.** A Host agent has reached the deep seams with an offer from the Heir: bread, names spoken at the Hearth, and a place in the new order for any Oathless smith who comes west. Some are listening. Torvek isn't, yet.
- **The empty chairs' holds.** The two lost clans left two dead holds. Their deep roads still connect to the living ones, and the Oathless use them. Nobody else goes there.
- **The Door (late-series hint).** From Act II, the forge-fires in the Great Hold lean toward the Sealed Door, as if drawn by a draught from below. The carvers have started working with their backs to the wall. See `world/geography.md`.

## Hooks for one-shots

- **The Doorstep (filler, Act I–II):** the autumn grain caravan arrives half-empty, and the dwarves accuse the carters of theft. The carters say the Host burned the rest. Someone is lying, and a winter depends on who.
- **The dead hold (filler, Act II):** the heroes are hired to escort a carver into one of the lost clans' holds to recover the clan's names before they're forgotten. The Oathless got there first.
- **The vouching (filler, any act):** getting into a hold needs a dwarf to vouch for you. The only one willing is an Oathless, and their vouch is worthless. Or is it?
- **The Moot (filler, Act II–III):** the vote to close the passes, with the heroes as the only outsiders in the room.

## Open questions for the DM

- The Doorstep, the Gate Stair, the feeding tunnel's route, the dead holds and the Host's offer to the Oathless are new. Keep, cut or rename?
- Name the other four holds and the lost clans now, or when a session needs them?
- The Host courting the Oathless sharpens the Pretender's existing plan for the Holds ("the Oathless as his smiths"). Does it step on The Forging?
