# The Ashen Frontier

Setting for The Pretender's Mask.

This is a **regional pass**. The Host, the Heir and his war plan are in `factions/ashen-pretender.md`; how the Frontier fits with the other regions is in `world/geography.md`. This file covers the ground.

## The land

The Frontier is Calderra's far west: the land the empire burned in its first wars and never rebuilt. Grey hills, dead forests standing like burnt matches, black-soil valleys where the old fires burned hottest, and rivers that run grey with ash. Along the ridges stand the empire's frontier forts, a chain of them, all ruined. Beyond the last hills is the western coast: cliffs, coves and grey surf.

Nothing here is new. Every road, wall and well was built by someone who left. That's the Frontier's character: a whole country of leftovers, which is why the people the empire threw away ended up here.

At its eastern edge the Frontier frays into Greenhollow's scrub. Hedgeend, the last Shire parish, burned this spring. To the north, the Thornwall runs down to the sea, and the strip of coast below its western end is where the clans' boats landed.

**What's changed since the emperor died:**
- **The Host rose.** A year ago the Frontier was scattered ruins and scattered people. Now its people are an army, and the army is moving east.
- **The forts are full again.** Ruined forts the empire abandoned a century ago have smoke rising from their courtyards. The Host camps beside them, never inside.
- **The money stopped.** Imperial coin used to reach the Frontier in one place only. It stopped when the emperor died, and that's when the Host began.
- **Every sun is upside down.** The Host has turned the imperial suns over on every fort gate. Travellers know they've crossed into the Heir's country when the suns fall.
- **The Frontier is emptying.** As the Host marches east, it takes almost everyone with it. Behind it is a land of ruins, goats and the very old.

## Places a party passes through

| Place | What it is | What a party finds there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Cinder Forts** | The chain of ruined imperial forts along the ridges, each a day apart. | Smoke-blackened walls, upside-down suns, Host companies camped outside them, and the ghosts locals swear by. |
| **The old battlefields** | Valleys where the first emperor's legions fought. | Rusted weapons on top, better ones buried deeper, and scavengers who know the difference. |
| **The black-soil valleys** | Valleys where the first fires burned hottest. The soil is black and strangely fertile. | The Frontier's only real farms: goats, roots, and ash-bread wheat. |
| **The grey rivers** | Rivers running grey with ash from the hills. | Drinkable if you strain it; locals won't swim in them. |
| **The western cove** | A nameless cove on the far western coast, below the cliffs. | Ships that don't fly a flag, and crates marked as grain. |
| **The broken road** | The old imperial road west from Greenhollow to the forts, its milestones knocked over and its bridges down. | The quickest way in, and the most watched. |
| **The Host's camp** | Not a place but a moving city of tents, wagons and fires, always in the open, never behind walls. In Act III it's two weeks from the capital, on the Frontier's eastern edge. | The Hearth, the one loaf, drums at dawn, a dozen peoples camped by clan, and a masked man carrying water. |

**Roads:** only the broken road east to Greenhollow. Everything else is goat tracks, ridgelines and the old fort-to-fort military road, which is half gone. The Host moves fast anyway, because it carries nothing it can't eat or burn. Travel times are in `world/geography.md`.

## The Frontier in its adventure

- **The Pretender's Mask (Act III):** the war camp on the Frontier's eastern edge, two weeks' march from Solace. The camp's layout follows the Host's rules: no walls; companies camped by clan; the Hearth fire at the centre, where the chiefs eat from one loaf; the Heir's tent beside it, plain and unguarded except by the Hearth. The adventure notes call for anti-scrying wards and a giant warlord or two.
- **Before Act III:** heroes rarely come here. The Frontier comes to them, as the Host's raiders. That's the point: by the time a party crosses the broken road, the players should already know what the Host is like.

## Secrets of the ground

- **The fort where they lived.** One Cinder Fort, the westernmost, sits on a ridge above the cliffs. Marit and Rowan lived in its gatehouse from their exile until her death. Marit's grave is in its courtyard, under an ash tree. It's where the Will gem appeared on the night the emperor died. The Host doesn't camp there; the Heir goes alone.
- **The garrison that doesn't exist.** The Quill's books record a frontier garrison at that same fort, paid every year as wall pay. A Quill auditor who came out to inspect it would find an empty gatehouse, a grave, and the Heir's childhood. Kestrel used to ride that route.
- **The cove is a two-way door.** Hargrove's ships land arms at the western cove; the Host's boats leave from it to carry clan warbands around the Thornwall. The cove has a lookout post on the cliffs above it, and a smugglers' path up to the old fort road.
- **The land remembers the Will.** The Will gem was taken from the far west. The first fires burned so hot here, and the land has stayed so dead, because the Will's crack is under these hills. From Act II, embers in century-old ash are still warm, and the grey rivers run warm in winter. Locals say the ground is angry. See `world/geography.md` and `sunfire-crown.md`.

## Hooks for one-shots

- **Hedgeend (filler, Act I–II):** the burned parish on the border. Survivors want their dead buried; a Host foraging party wants the last grain store; the heroes are between them.
- **The battlefield (filler, Act II):** scavengers have dug up something from the first wars that both the Host and the Temple want. Nobody remembers who buried it, or why.
- **The cove (filler, Act II):** if the Black Gull's log was played with Option B, the heroes can go to the cove itself, watch a delivery, and decide what to do about it.
- **The empty Frontier (Act III, after The Mask):** with the Host gone east, the old and the very young are left alone among the ruins. A small, kind one-shot before the finale.
