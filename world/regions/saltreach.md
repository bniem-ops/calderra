# The Saltreach Coast

Setting for The Black Gull.

This is a **regional pass**. The Reachers, the Tide Table, the Old Houses and their secrets are in `factions/saltreach.md`; how the coast fits with the other regions is in `world/geography.md`. This file covers the ground.

## The land

The Reach is Calderra's eastern seaboard. It runs from the Ironspine's foothills in the north, where the mountains come down to the sea in cliffs, to the edge of the Delta's mangroves in the south. Between them: deep harbours cut into rocky headlands, salt marsh and salt pans along the flats, and the eastern pine forests climbing inland toward the mountains. A Reacher town is a harbour first and a town second: everything faces the water.

The Reach is narrow. A day's ride inland and you're in the pines; two and you're in the Ironspine's foothills. The sea is the real country. Reachers think of the coast as one long street, with the harbours as its doors.

**What's changed since the emperor died:**
- **The navy went pirate.** The imperial fleet was based at Anchorhead and went unpaid when the emperor died. Some ships went home; some sold themselves to the Compact; the worst, led by the Black Gull, now take what they want.
- **The sun-marks came off.** The week the Compact was signed, every harbour chiselled the imperial sun off its customs house and carved the **Compact's knot** in its place. The scars are still fresh.
- **Prices on every board.** With no imperial tariffs, every harbour sets its own prices, chalked on slate boards along the docks and changed by the hour. Reachers love it. Everyone else finds it exhausting.
- **The Wreck Walls are getting longer.** A war ruins people, and a ruined debtor's name goes on the wall.
- **Grain in, grain out.** Reacher ships are buying grain in Greenhollow and the Delta, carrying it everywhere, and smuggling some of it into Solace for the unlicensed bakers.

## Places a party passes through

| Place | What it is | What a party finds there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Highwater** | The chief port, at the middle of the coast. The Tide Table meets in **the Tide Hall** on the harbour front, a long timber hall with a seat for every ship-owner. | Odessa Reef, the Old Houses' townhouses, the biggest Wreck Wall in the Reach, and a hundred ships at anchor. |
| **The harbour taverns** | Every port has a row of them along the waterfront. The Black Gull's recruitment scenes start here. | Shanties, dice, rum, bond-sailors looking for a better master, and gossip about every ship at sea. |
| **Anchorhead** | The old imperial navy yard at the coast's southern end: dry docks, rope-walks, a fortress, and empty berths. | Unpaid sailors, a few rotting hulls, and the Black Gull's old berth, still marked with her name. |
| **The Ribyards** | The shipyards in the pine country behind the northern harbours, where Reacher hulls are built. | Shipwrights, timber rafts and new hulls going up fast, because everyone wants a ship this year. |
| **The salt flats** | Salt marsh and salt pans along the southern coast, worked by bond-sailors at noon. | Heat, glare, white heaps of salt, and the worst work in the Reach. |
| **The Lights** | A chain of lighthouses along the headlands, one in sight of the next. | Keepers who know every ship that passes, and who is paying them to forget one. |
| **The Teeth** | A field of rocks and reefs off the southern headlands, toward the Delta. | Wrecks, fog and the Black Gull's favourite hunting ground. |
| **The fishing villages** | Small harbours between the big ones, with their own boats and salt pans. | Free captains, Odessa's voters, and people who remember the Reach before it was rich. |

**Roads:** the Reach travels by sea. On land, the **east road** runs from Highwater over the hills to Solace, and the **ore road** climbs north-west to the Ironspine's Doorstep. The **southern lanes** past the Teeth lead to Vareth and the Delta's mouths, which Reachers and Vareth both claim. Travel times are in `world/geography.md`.

**The Lights and the Thornwall's beacons** are mirror images: one chain of fire to warn of enemies, one to guide ships home. A Marcher and a Reacher could argue for an hour about which matters more.

## The coast in its adventure

- **The Black Gull (Act I):** recruitment in a Highwater tavern; the chase out past the Lights toward the Teeth (the battle map's rocks); the boarding; the captain's log. Option A's Vareth prince ties the adventure to the Delta's toll on the southern lanes. Option B's log points west, to a cove on the Frontier coast.
- **Later visits:** at Act II levels, the Tide Hall is a ready-made setting for an intrigue one-shot. At Act III levels, a sea battle off Highwater or the Teeth while the Host's war reaches the coast.

## Secrets of the ground

- **The Lights see everything.** The lighthouse keepers log every ship that passes. The keeper at the southernmost Light has been paid by House Hargrove for two years to leave certain ships out of the log: the ones heading south around the continent toward the western cove. The keeper's honest second log is hidden under the lamp floor.
- **Anchorhead's fortress.** The imperial navy's records are still in the Anchorhead fortress, including the Black Gull's last orders and pay ledgers. They show the navy's pay was stopped by Regency order before the emperor died, not after, and the order was signed with the Quiet Seal. The Gull's crew has a real grievance against Solace, and the proof is sitting in an empty fort.
- **The Teeth hide a channel.** The Black Gull uses a deep channel through the Teeth that no Reacher chart shows. It's how she vanishes. Captain Vane learned it from a smuggler named Odessa Reef, twenty years ago.
- **Ghost ships (late-series hint).** From Act II, fog off the Teeth brings ghost ships nobody's ledger can account for, flying no flag in Calderra. Reachers throw coins over the side, and the coins don't sink. See `world/geography.md`.

## Hooks for one-shots

- **The regatta (filler, Act I–II):** Highwater's spring regatta, with a ship as the prize. Every claimant has entered a crew, and one of the boats is carrying something that isn't for racing.
- **The Wreck Wall (filler, any act):** a name is painted on the wall overnight, and the merchant it names swears he paid. If he's lying, he's ruined; if he isn't, someone is forging debts.
- **Anchorhead (filler, Act II):** the navy yard's last loyal officer offers the heroes the fortress's records, if they can get them out before the Old Houses burn them.
- **The salt flats (filler, Act II–III):** the bond-sailors walk off the pans, and the Tide Table wants the heroes to talk them back. The sailors want the heroes to carry their demands to the Table.

## Open questions for the DM

- Highwater, the Tide Hall, Anchorhead, the Ribyards, the Lights and the Teeth are new names. Keep, cut or rename?
- The Anchorhead pay-order secret gives the Black Gull's crew a grievance against Solace. It fits both twist options. Too much?
- Is Odessa teaching Vane the Teeth channel too neat? Cut it if their relationship should stay unconnected.
