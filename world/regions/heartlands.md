# The Heartlands

The capital, Solace, sits here. Settings: The Feast of Banners (at the Lanternhold), The Regent's Vault and The Council of Banners.

This is a **regional pass**, not a faction pass. The people, the court, the Granaries and the Regent's secrets are in `factions/regency.md`; the Lanternhold and the Order are in `factions/dawn-lantern.md`. This file covers the ground: the places a party walks through, the roads, and what the fall of the empire has done to them.

## The land

The Heartlands are the middle of Calderra, and every road in the continent ends here. Low hills of vineyards and olive groves, marble quarries cut into the ridges, and big Sunward country estates worked by tenant farmers, all running down to a broad bay where Solace sits.

It's rich land, but not rich enough. The Heartlands grow wine, oil and stone, and Solace eats more bread than they could ever grow. Every loaf in the city comes in along the grain road from Greenhollow, or by ship.

**What's changed since the emperor died:**
- **The legions went home.** The barracks outside the walls are empty. The city watch and the Sunward Houses' hired swords are all that's left, and they don't trust each other.
- **The roads are emptier.** Imperial road wardens stopped being paid. Inside a day's ride of Solace the roads are still safe; beyond that, toll-keepers and bandits are moving in.
- **Strangers everywhere.** Every claimant keeps an embassy in Solace, and every embassy keeps spies. Reacher ships crowd the harbour. Ironhart soldiers drink in the lower city on leave. Dwarven factors haggle at the mint.
- **The Morning Price is climbing,** and the crowd at the palace steps at dawn gets bigger and quieter every week.
- **Sun-marks are being smashed** at night, and the Quill can't catch who's doing it.

## Solace

A city of white marble, gold domes and masks, built in rings around Palace Hill and down to the harbour. Beautiful, crowded and afraid.

| District | What it is | What a party finds there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Palace Hill** | The imperial palace under the great dome, where the Temple's eternal flame burns. The throne room, with the replica crown on its cushion. The palace steps, where the Morning Price is read at dawn. | Galas, petitions, guards everywhere, and a vault level nobody talks about. |
| **The Hall of Banners** | A long hall below the palace, hung with the banner of every province and people that ever swore to the Crown. By imperial law the Council meets here. | Empty and echoing for now. Workmen are re-hanging banners; someone keeps arguing over which ones. |
| **The Quill** | The civil service's offices and the Hall of Records: miles of shelves, three centuries of paper, nothing ever burned. | Clerks who know everything and say nothing, and the exams where a nobody can rise. |
| **The Sunward quarter** | Marble townhouses of the old noble families, walled gardens, private chapels. | Masked dinners, hired guards in house colours, Lady Corisande's salon. |
| **The baths** | The great public baths. Where Solace's real politics happens, in steam, without masks. | Rumours, deals, and the safest place in the city to be overheard. |
| **The Granary quarter** | The city granaries and imperial mills along the harbour, behind their own wall. | Quill guards, sacks counted twice a day, and a smell of flour on everything. |
| **The lower city** | Docks, tenements, wine shops and the unlicensed bakers' ovens. | Reacher sailors, Ironhart soldiers on leave, smashed sun-marks, and the first place a riot starts. |
| **The harbour** | A deep harbour on the bay, crowded with Reacher hulls. | Everything Solace imports comes through here, on someone else's ships. |
| **The pyre hill** | A bare hill outside the west gate where the dead are burned at sunset. | Quiet; the only place in Solace where people don't wear masks in public. |

**Gates and roads:** the **west gate** opens on the grain road to Greenhollow; the **north gate** on the road to the March; the **east road** runs to the Saltreach coast; the **south road** leads to the Lanternhold and, past it, the Gilded Delta.

## Around Solace

- **The Lanternhold.** The Order's hall: a lone castle on a hill half a day's ride south of the city, with no town around it and no farmland. The only neutral ground left. See `factions/dawn-lantern.md`.
- **The vineyard estates.** Sunward country houses on the slopes north and east of the city, each with its own village of tenants. Several have been quietly fortified since spring.
- **The marble quarries.** Cut into the ridges west of the city. Since the legions left, quarry gangs have been hiring themselves out as muscle.
- **The coaching inns.** A ring of big imperial inns, each a day's ride from Solace on every road. Every traveller to the capital stops at one the night before; every claimant's spies know it.

**Travel times:** Solace to the Lanternhold, half a day; to Wendle Cross in Greenhollow, five days by cart; to the Saltreach coast, about four days by road or two by sea; to the Thornwall, about eight days north.

## The Heartlands in each adventure

- **The Feast of Banners (Act II).** Every claimant rides out of Solace down the south road to the Lanternhold, each with an escort, each stopping at the same coaching inn the night before. A good place for a cold open, or for the heroes to meet the guests before the gates lock.
- **The Regent's Vault (Act II).** The way in is a palace gala on Palace Hill: masks, music, the Sunward Houses and every embassy in one room. The vault lies below the palace. (The battle-map prompt in `world/maps.md` gives it a guard room, three locked chambers, a service corridor and a hidden drain exit.)
- **The Council of Banners (Act III).** The Council sits in the Hall of Banners. When the Pretender moves, the fighting runs from the west gate along the grain-road avenue, through the lower city and up Palace Hill to the throne room. Allied factions arrive by the gates they'd use: Ironhart by the north gate, Reacher ships into the harbour, the Holds' column down the grain road behind the Host.

## Secrets of the ground

- **The vault drain.** The hidden drain exit from the vault joins the old imperial drains, which run under Palace Hill and out to the harbour. The bread smugglers who supply the unlicensed bakers use the outer drains to move grain into the lower city at night. They know the tunnels better than the Quill does, and they don't know how close they come to the vault. A party that befriends the bakers gets a map, and a way out.
- **The Granary quarter is nearly empty.** The city granaries are in the same state as the Long Barns in Greenhollow: the palace tally says half full; the floors say a quarter. Lysander knows. A riot that broke in would find out in minutes, and so would the whole city. See `regions/greenhollow.md` and the Solari private hook "The Granary count."
- **Who's smashing the sun-marks.** Not one group. Some are angry lower-city youths; some are Kestrel's agents, preparing the city to open its gates to a true heir; and a few are Temple zealots, who say the marks are a lie while the Sun has no crown. The Quill assumes it's all one conspiracy and is hunting the wrong people.
- **The Hall of Banners has a missing banner.** When the hall was re-hung this year, the workmen found an empty hook between the March and Greenhollow, with a scorched space on the wall. It belonged to the western province, taken down when the empire burned the west in its first wars, and never hung again. Kestrel, who spent years in the Quill, knows the story. If the Heir takes the city, that banner goes back up first, and the Host will carry the old one rather than his upside-down sun.

## Hooks for one-shots

- **The Morning Price (filler, Act I–II):** the price jumps two coppers at dawn and the lower city boils over. The heroes are caught between the watch, the crowd and an unlicensed baker everyone wants to hang, and someone is steering the riot toward the Granary quarter.
- **The coaching inn (filler, Act II):** three claimants' embassies stuck at the same snowed-in inn the night before they reach Solace. One of them won't arrive.
- **The drains (filler, Act II, or a lead-in to The Regent's Vault):** the bread smugglers' runner has gone missing in the tunnels under Palace Hill, and something down there is guarding a door.
- **The re-hanging (filler, Act III):** the Quill hires the heroes to guard the Hall of Banners for a week before the Council. Every faction wants its banner hung higher, and someone wants one torn down.
