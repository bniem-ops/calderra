# Calderra — Premise

> DM-only canon. Nothing in `world/` is shown on the player map unless it is copied into a public field.

Calderra is a continent in the first year after its empire fell, and every one-shot is a chance for the heroes to decide what rises in its place. Millhaven and its Fey Accord are one small village in one province: a local legend, not the main story.

## The premise

Six months ago, Emperor Castellan VII, called the Last Sun, died without an heir. On the night he died, the **Sunfire Crown** shattered into three gems and vanished. The imperial legions went home, the roads went quiet, and every duke, guild and warlord with an army started calling themselves a rightful successor. People call this the **Age of Banners**.

## The clock

By imperial law, a new ruler is recognised at the **Council of Banners**, held in the capital one year after an emperor's death. Whoever arrives holding the Crown (or enough of it, or enough allies) will shape the continent. That gives the series its arc: a run of one-shots over one in-world year, then a finale.

**The big question for the table:** Who should wear the Sunfire Crown, and will the heroes let them, take it themselves, or make sure no one does?

## Tone pillars

1. **Heroes in the gap.** The empire's protection is gone. Monsters raid roads, bandits rule bridges, and the people who step up are adventurers.
2. **Big, clear villains; grey politics.** The monsters and warlords are worth punching. The claimants are human: each has a fair point and a flaw.
3. **Every place is different.** Seven regions, seven flavours: siege warfare, sea chases, dragon hunts, courtly intrigue, village festivals.
4. **Choices stick.** Nothing resets. Who the heroes help this session shows up, grateful or angry, in a later one.

## Inspirations

The Lord of the Rings for heroic last stands, Game of Thrones (minus the cruelty) for rival claimants, Pirates of the Caribbean for the coast, The Princess Bride for swashbuckling banter, and Festival of the Accord for village-scale trials.

## Buried payoff (don't use for a while)

The Sunfire Crown wasn't only a symbol. Its three gems held shut old cracks to other worlds, which is why strange things (Fey Accords, psionic plant monsters) have started leaking into the provinces since it broke. Drop a hint now and then. Reveal it late, once the players have a reason to care.
