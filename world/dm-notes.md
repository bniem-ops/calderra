# DM notes: what to keep and what to sharpen

Based on Festival of the Accord. The biggest upgrades for a shared world are more player choices, fewer stacked rolls, and spotlights that fit whoever shows up.

## Keep doing

- **Every scene pays off later.** Revel rewards fed directly into the boss fight. Do the same across sessions with the ledger.
- **Foreshadowing in three voices.** SERA, MAX and the Engulf tell all taught the same lesson.
- **A warm anchor NPC.** Every one-shot should have a MAX.
- **Graded outcomes.** Full, partial and failure results keep the story moving.
- **Failure is a story, not a game over.**

## Story: improve

1. **Give players real decisions.** At least two meaningful choices per one-shot (who to trust, who to save, take the deal or not). Choices are what make the ledger work.
2. **Put the villain on stage early.** A voice or face in the first act (a herald, a threat, a demand).
3. **Trim and separate NPC names.** Three or four named NPCs per one-shot, each with a different first letter and one memorable trait. (ALDRIC and ALDREN in the same session will get mixed up.)
4. **Let players bring their own motives.** Ask each player one question before the session and weave one answer in.

## Structure: improve

1. **Avoid stacked "both must succeed" checks.** Two checks at ~70% and ~65% pass together only ~45% of the time. Use success at a cost or a three-step progress track.
2. **Write spotlights by role, not class:** the face, the brain, the sneak, the muscle, the support. With rotating casts you won't know who's coming.
3. **Give spectators a job** in every solo scene: help, sabotage or bet.
4. **Watch the reward stack.** One big reward per scene, or scale the boss HP to rewards earned.
5. **Finish loose rules before printing** (e.g. "HP set by Revel Four outcome" with no rule for how).

## Cipher fix (if reused)

- Plaintext FLEE AT ONCE, padded with X: FLEEATONCEXX
- Grid under M A X: FLE / EAT / ONC / EXX
- Read columns alphabetically (A, M, X): LANX + FEOE + ETCX
- **Ciphertext: LANXFEOEETCX**
- Decode: split into three equal chunks; chunk 1 under **A**, chunk 2 under **M**, chunk 3 under **X**; read across rows.

## One-shot template

1. **Hook** (10 min): an Order of the Dawn Lantern request or a player's personal motive.
2. **The face of the problem** (20 min): meet the villain or their herald.
3. **Three set-pieces** (2 hours): one social, one exploration or puzzle, one action, each with a spotlight role and a job for everyone else.
4. **The choice** (15 min): a decision with no perfect answer.
5. **The climax** (45 min): a fight whose difficulty reflects earlier choices.
6. **The ripple** (5 min): read what changed in Calderra, then log the session.
