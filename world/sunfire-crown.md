# The Sunfire Crown

Shattered into three gems the night Emperor Castellan VII died. Current locations and who knows live in `ledger/state.yaml`; this file holds the lore.

- **The Heart gem:** hidden by Regent Lysander under the palace in Solace, to stop anyone taking the throne early.
- **The Will gem:** carried by the Ashen Pretender. Only his herald Kestrel knows.
- **The Mind gem:** lost, carried off by whatever crack it was sealing. It will turn up somewhere strange.

What the broken Crown means for law, faith and magic is in `premise.md` ("The broken Crown").
