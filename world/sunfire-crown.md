# The Sunfire Crown

> DM-only. The public knows only the first section. Current gem locations live in `ledger/state.yaml`.

## What everyone knows

On the night Emperor Castellan VII, the Last Sun, died, **the Sunfire Crown vanished from his brow.** Witnesses say the gold turned to light and was gone. The Temple says the Sun took his crown with him when he set. Rumour says its three gems survived, and scattered.

The fake crown on a cushion in the throne room is exactly that: a replica Lysander had made so the court would have something to bow to.

## What the Crown really was

**A seal.** Three hundred years ago, the first emperor found three stones at the three places where the world was thinnest, where other worlds pressed through. They were set into one crown, forged in dragonfire at the heart of **Mount Kharag**, and the cracks closed. That is where the name *Sunfire* comes from. It is also the true reason the empire paid the dragon of Kharag for three hundred years: she was the Crown's **forge-keeper**, bound by the first treaty to keep the forge-heart burning and to sleep beside it. (The empire's public reason, "to keep the dragon asleep," was half the truth.)

### The three gems

Each gem is a gift. Inside the Crown, the other two balanced it. **Alone, each one has a cost.**

| Gem | Taken from | What it gives | Its cost when alone | Where it went |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Heart** | Solace, where "the light first touched the ground" | **Bonds.** Oaths sworn to the Crown truly held. This is the magic behind the Crown as Calderra's referee. | Binds its bearer to their deepest promise. They cannot let it go. | Caught by Lysander at the deathbed |
| **The Will** | The far west, now the Ashen Frontier | **Drive.** Courage, command, followers who don't break | With nothing to temper it, it burns its bearer up from the inside | Marit's grave, where Rowan found it |
| **The Mind** | The Fey border in Greenhollow, near Millhaven | **Sight.** Truth, visions, other minds | Opens the bearer to other worlds: madness, and strange psychic power | Fell through the Fey crack, into the Verdant Court's Challenger |

### The rule: each gem went home

When the Crown set with the Sun, each gem returned to the place it was taken from, and to whoever there was bound to it:
- **The Heart** stayed in Solace, with the man keeping the emperor's last promise.
- **The Will** went west, to the grave of the woman who had saved everything for her son, and to the son who swore over it.
- **The Mind** went back through the crack it had sealed at Greenhollow. The Verdant Court's Challenger grew around it, which is why the Shambling Mound at the **Festival of the Accord** had an alien, psionic mind. When the heroes destroyed it, the gem stayed in its dead roots, under the Millhaven coliseum.

### Why the gems behave as they do now

- **Lysander and the Heart:** at the bedside he promised the dying emperor he would keep the empire whole. The Heart holds him to it. It is why he cannot stop lying to protect the empire, and why he cannot accept that it might end.
- **Rowan and the Will:** fed by his grief and his vow at his mother's grave, with no Heart to give it purpose and no Mind to restrain it, the Will gives him and the Host an iron will, and burns him. Grey ash is spreading from his chest. He may have a year or two.
- **The Mind, unheld:** in the roots under the coliseum, it leaks. Millhaven's children have started having the same dreams.

### The Last Sun's madness

The **sun-sickness** of the eight dark years was the Crown failing. After forty years on one brow, with the cracks pressing harder every year, the Mind gem's pressure broke the emperor's mind. The dark years were the first sign that the seal was wearing out, and the Crown's vanishing at his death was the seal finally giving way.

### What the broken seal means

With the gems apart, the cracks are slowly opening. Fey Accords like Millhaven's are fraying; strange creatures turn up where they shouldn't; old seals like the Holdfast's Sealed Door feel thinner. This stays in the background until late in the series.

## Making a new crown

The old Crown is gone. **A new one has to be forged**, in the same place and the same way. It needs:

1. **All three gems.**
2. **A Holdfast master smith.** The best living is **Torvek Nameless**, struck from his clan for refusing to forge the fake tribute seals. He'll do it for his name back.
3. **Dragonfire at Kharag's forge-heart.** Kharagax is dead; her hatchling is the only dragon who can light it. Kill the young dragon and the forge goes cold for a generation.

That makes the dwarves suddenly central: **they hold the forge and the smith.** And it means the Dragon of Mount Kharag one-shot decides whether a crown can be made at all.

### What a new crown does

- **Whole, it seals the cracks again**, whether or not anyone wears it.
- **Worn, it gives all three gifts** (bonds, drive, sight) in balance, and the legitimacy every claimant wants.
- **Set together, the gems stop costing their bearers.** The Will stops burning Rowan; the Heart lets Lysander go.
- **Blood does not matter.** The Temple teaches that only a blood heir can wear it. That's untrue: anyone can. The Will went to Rowan because of his vow, not his blood.

## How the gems come together across the series

Gems are held by **factions between sessions, never by PCs.** The Order of the Dawn Lantern is the natural keeper of any gem the heroes secure. Each gem's cost falls on whoever holds it, so every handoff is a story. Track holders in `ledger/state.yaml`.

| I | Harrow's Ford (4), The Black Gull (5) | No gems. The war and the Host's raiders. |

### Getting the Will gem

The Pretender wants a crown too, so he will bring the Will gem to the end himself. What the heroes did in **The Pretender's Mask** decides how:
1. **Stolen:** the Host loses its iron will and starts to splinter; the burn stops; Rowan is weaker but alive, and furious.
2. **Turned:** shown the lockbox, his uncle MAX and the acknowledgement, and told the truth (the crown is the only thing that stops the burn), he brings the Will gem to the Forging as an ally.
3. **Left:** he arrives at the Forging with the gem and an army, to take the crown for himself.

### If a gem is lost

If the heroes fail to secure a gem, it goes to another faction: the Temple, the Verdant Court, Hargrove or the Host. The Forging and the Council then play out with that faction holding a piece of the crown, and a price for it.

## The ending

Once a new crown exists, the Council of Banners decides what happens to it. There is no fixed answer: whoever wears it, keeps it or refuses it, the players at the table choose. Some ways it could go:
- **Crowned:** Brenna, Leo, Rowan or someone unexpected wears it. One empire again.
- **Kept, not worn:** the crown seals the cracks from a keeper's hands (the Hall of Names, the Order, the Temple) and the peoples of Calderra go their own ways.
- **Never forged, or broken again:** the cracks keep widening. That is the epilogue, with the Sealed Door and the Fey.
